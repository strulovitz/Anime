SPECIES REFERENCE SHEET, clean neutral warm-grey studio background, soft even lighting, crisp focus everywhere — master visual reference for the TITANITES, a fully NON-HUMANOID alien species. CRITICAL: no bipedal stance, no human torso or face, no resemblance to humans, the Zĭrán, or the Ondines — an entirely independent evolutionary line from a brutal high-gravity world.

SHOW: (1) one adult Titanite full-body in three-quarter view standing at rest, (2) the same adult in side profile, (3) a close-up detail panel of the sensory brow, eyes and tool-limbs, (4) a vignette of the ceremonial salute — one tool-limb pressed flat against the chest-plate, head lowered. Include a faint human silhouette outline for scale: an adult Titanite stands about shoulder-height to a human but is over three meters long and masses like a small truck.

EVOLUTIONARY LOGIC (drives every detail): the Titanites evolved on a world with THREE TIMES Earth's gravity. There, a fall of half a meter kills; nothing tall, thin or fragile survives. Evolution answered with width, weight, armor and patience — and with a voice made of thunder, because in dense heavy air, deep sound travels farther than sight.

BODY PLAN: a living fortress — a low, broad, dome-backed body carried on EIGHT massive pillar-legs arranged in two rows of four, each leg short, thick as a tree trunk, ending in a wide cushioned foot-pad with anchoring claws; the gait is a slow, unstoppable, earthquake-steady ripple (four feet always planted). At the front, the dome tapers into a blunt armored PROW — not a raised head; nothing on this world lifts its head — housing the senses low and safe. Folded beneath the prow: TWO heavy TOOL-LIMBS, thick and elbowed like a mantis built by a blacksmith, each ending in a three-pronged gripper of shocking strength — and, nested inside each gripper, a bundle of six short fine feeler-digits for delicate work: forging, engraving, tending seedlings. The strongest hands in the galaxy, and among the gentlest.

ARMOR — their glory: the entire back and flanks are covered in BIOMINERALIZED PLATES — the creature grows living rock: overlapping slabs of slate-grey and iron-blue mineral, hexagonal-tending, weathered and lichen-flecked like an old mountainside, with visible growth rings on each plate telling the individual's age. Between the plates, seams of tough leathery hide, deep charcoal. Old Titanites are literally craggier. Show one detail inset of a single plate with growth rings.

SENSES & THE FACE THAT IS NOT A FACE: beneath a jutting mineral BROW-SHELF at the prow sit FOUR EYES in a low horizontal row — small relative to the body but deeply expressive: dark amber, wet, patient, heavy-lidded, with a slow deliberate blink that is their species' smile. No visible nose or outer ears: they hear through their legs — seismic receptors in the foot-pads read the ground, so a Titanite feels your footsteps and your heartbeat through the floor. Below the brow, a wide grazing mouth kept politely closed, framed by two short sensory whiskers of braided horn.

VOICE: a great RESONANCE CHAMBER inside the dome-back produces their speech — subsonic rumble-language that listeners feel in the chest before they hear it; when a Titanite speaks, dust dances on the floor. Show a subtle annotation: faint concentric vibration rings at the feet during speech.

CULTURE ON THE BODY: Titanites wear no clothes — instead their plates are ENGRAVED: fine chiseled scrollwork and honor-marks carved into their own living rock by clan artisans, inlaid with threads of meteor-metal. A heavy woven strap across the prow carries forge-tools and a small hammer worn smooth by generations. Their salute: one mighty gripper pressed flat over the chest-plate, all four eyes closed, head lowered — strength choosing reverence.

MOOD OF THE DESIGN: immovable gentleness — a mountain that learned kindness; slow, courteous, devastating only when the innocent are threatened. Pixar-level appeal through the patient amber eyes, the slow blink and the careful way something so huge moves around something so small — never through human features. Realistic bio-materials, hard-science-fiction plausibility. Reference sheet: no dramatic shadows, no background scenery.
