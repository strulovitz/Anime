# Entity #89 — Wooden Temple Bell
## By Claude Fable 5 — 2026-07-27

SUBJECT: the great WOODEN TEMPLE BELL of the Zĭrán, rung in alarm at night as blazing meteor trails streak the sky. Single full-frame reference image: the bell and its ringer, three-quarter view, at the moment of a mighty swing.

THE BELL: a huge temple bell carved from a SINGLE massive tree trunk — not metal, wood: deep honey-brown heartwood with visible growth rings on its lower rim, its outer surface carved with concentric bands of flowing Zĭrán relief patterns, polished to a soft sheen by centuries of ceremonial use. It hangs from a thick living BRANCH ARCH — part of a growing tree trained over generations into a perfect bell frame, leaves still sprouting from the arch — lashed with braided cord. A heavy braided bell-rope of plant fiber hangs from the striker mechanism. The bell is caught MID-SWING, tilted, a deep resonant boom almost visible in the trembling air.

THE RINGER — anatomy lock, absolutely non-humanoid: SHAOZU, a young Zĭrán monk — a low six-limbed creature like a living mangrove root, about 1.2 meters tall: FOUR sturdy column-like walking limbs with wide splayed grip-pads braced hard against the ground, and TWO long boneless trunk-like manipulator arms (like elephant trunks ending in three finger-tendrils each) both wrapped around the bell-rope, hauling with all his strength, his whole body straining backward. His smooth dappled skin (olive-green, bronze and cream patches of symbiotic algae) flares with quick bright expanding rings of urgency. His swan-neck arc rises to a sensory crown of five flexible frond-stalks — three eye-stalks with amber-ring eyes all converged upward at the sky, two feather-edged acoustic fronds flattened back. A simple woven moss-fiber mantle across his back. NO face, NO human features, NO bipedal stance.

SKY AND LIGHT: night sky above the bell arch torn by several blazing meteor trails — brilliant white-orange streaks with glowing smoke tails, descending at a steep angle. Their harsh light mixes with the warm teal-and-gold glow of bioluminescent flowers hanging near the bell platform. Long urgent shadows.

MOOD: the night the sky fell — one small brave monk waking his whole world.

FORBIDDEN: no metal bell, no humanoid ringer, no temple buildings in frame — the bell, the living arch, Shaozu, and the burning sky.
