# Entity #91 — Silicate Spires Reef
## By Claude Fable 5 — 2026-07-27

SUBJECT: a reef of pale SILICATE SPIRES on the sea floor of Ross 128 b — coral made of glass, a natural crystal architecture grown by living organisms. Single full-frame underwater reference image: wide view across the reef, low camera angle looking slightly upward so the spires tower against the bright water above.

WATER AND LIGHT: warm turquoise shallow sea, salmon-pink god-rays from an orange-red dwarf star slanting through the water and passing THROUGH the translucent spires, which light up from within like cathedral windows. Sparkling suspended particles in the beams.

THE SPIRES — hard-science biology: colonies of reef-building organisms that secrete SILICA instead of calcium carbonate (like Earth's glass sponges, scaled up over millions of years). The result: clusters of slender, tapering towers of pale translucent glass, from human-height up to ten meters tall, rising in gothic groups from the sea floor. Each spire's surface shows fine structural detail: delicate lattice ribbing, faint growth-band rings, frosted-glass texture on older lower sections grading to clearer, newer glass at the growing tips. Some spires branch like candelabra; a few ancient ones have fused into arches. Small broken stumps and glittering glass rubble at their bases tell of storms survived.

COLOR: the glass itself is nearly colorless — all its beauty comes from the light: it refracts the salmon rays into soft prismatic edges (subtle, physically plausible dispersion — NOT rainbow kitsch), glows amber-rose where the sun passes through, and shades to cool blue-green in shadow. Sparse patches of tiny indigo-black encrusting plants (dark pigments evolved for red-dwarf light) grow on the older surfaces, giving contrast.

COMPOSITION: spires arranged in receding clusters for depth — sharp detailed foreground group, mid-ground cluster, distant groups fading into turquoise haze. Sea floor of pale rippled sand with scattered glass fragments catching light.

FORBIDDEN: no Earth coral shapes (no brain coral, no branching staghorn), no saturated rainbow colors, no fish or creatures in frame — architecture of glass and light only.
