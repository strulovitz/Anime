ENTITY #23 — THE PROXIMA LICHEN CONSTELLATION (Scene 4)

Reference design sheet: the PROXIMA b LICHEN — the first alien life humanity ever touched, and quietly one of the toughest organisms in the galaxy. Surface panel: low glassy-crusted rosettes of cyan-glowing growth spreading across red rock in the eternal twilight of the tidally-locked terminator zone — the glow is luciferin-class bioluminescence, and the glassy crust is grown radiation shielding: a transparent mineral armor against the flare star's tantrums, evolved sunscreen. Subsurface cutaway panel (the revelation): beneath every small surface rosette, a vast buried MYCELIUM NETWORK spreads for kilometers — glowing threads linking rosette to rosette like a buried constellation, one organism the size of a county, softly pulsing where nutrients and signals travel. Detail study: a human gloved fingertip beside one rosette for scale, the glow reflecting on the glove.
