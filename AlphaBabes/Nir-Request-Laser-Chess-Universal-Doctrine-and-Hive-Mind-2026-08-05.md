   - Each worker AI returns only its own single best/"champion" answer for its slice — not the full search space, just its conclusion.
   - The Alpha (the crew's own ship computer) acts as the "Queen Bee" of this hive: it doesn't need to solve the whole gigantic combinatorial problem itself — it only needs to choose among the small number of already-optimized "champion" answers submitted by the worker ships, the way a Miss Universe judge doesn't have to evaluate every woman on Earth, only choose among candidates who already won their own country's pageant.

This forces Medusa to prune/trim unpromising branches early just to keep up — whereas the girls' hive doesn't need to prune, because the work is genuinely distributed across many independent small AIs computing in parallel with no cross-talk needed.
   - The hive is not as powerful as Medusa overall — Medusa is still stronger and still wins any direct confrontation — but the hive is powerful enough to "give Medusa a run for her money," which is the whole point: clever distributed architecture partially compensating for a raw power deficit.
