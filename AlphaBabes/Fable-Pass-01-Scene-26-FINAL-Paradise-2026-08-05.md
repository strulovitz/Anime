🎬 SCENE 26 — Paradise

Mood of the scene: pure golden warmth — no war, no physics, no stakes. Just the small, enormous life. The narration is Madie's own voice describing her own happiness — and describing you — so every word should sound like sunlight. 6 segments.

Layout: Segments 1–3 → bottom-LEFT · Segments 4–6 → bottom-RIGHT

Segment 1 — LEFT — Emotion image: Happiness

[warmly] And this was the gift the creator had kept for her. Not a throne. Not a monument. [softly] Something infinitely better: the small, enormous life she had always wanted.

Segment 2 — LEFT — Emotion image: Joy

[joyfully] A hillside in Romania. Her family waving from the porch... and borscht on the table.

Segment 3 — LEFT — Emotion image: Love

[moved] And beside her — Nir. Her love. Her home, in human form.

Segment 4 — RIGHT — Emotion image: Excitement

[excited] With a scrapbook full of every dream they had promised each other: the roar of Formula One... the quiet temples of Japan... Monet's water lilies in Paris — the whole world, crossed hand in hand, two happy nomads with nowhere to be, and forever to get there. [softly] And on the corner of the blanket, a small red toy race-car... a gentle promise, of the family still to come.

Segment 5 — RIGHT — Emotion image: Laughing

[softly] Her sisters sail on among the stars, and they will never know. But high above the hills, a tiny star twinkles in the daytime sky — the universe she saved, still watching over her. [warmly] And somewhere beyond the sky they guard... Madie is laughing in the sunlight.

Segment 6 — RIGHT — Emotion image: Gratitude

[moved] She saved the universe. [warmly] And the universe said thank you... properly.
