# Entity #103 — Anti-Missile Micro-Drone Clouds
## By Claude Fable 5 — 2026-07-27

SUBJECT: the Alpha's ANTI-MISSILE MICRO-DRONE CLOUD — thousands of expendable fist-sized drones deployed as a living shield between the mothership and incoming fire. Single full-frame reference image: the cloud in space, seen from just outside its edge, with one interception happening inside it.

THE INDIVIDUAL DRONE — shown in sharp detail in the foreground, close to camera: a fist-sized octahedral body of dull grey alloy, every face a flat plate carrying either a tiny cold-gas thruster nozzle (eight of them, one per vertex, for instant omnidirectional maneuvering), a small sensor window, or a fold-flat radar reflector vane. No engine glow — cold-gas puffs only, visible as faint brief crystalline plumes. A stenciled unit number and a single small green status LED. It is cheap, mass-produced, and knows it will die: no armor, no polish, just enough machine to be in the right place once.

THE CLOUD — collective behavior, physically true: thousands of identical drones dispersed in a vast three-dimensional screen filling the middle distance — not a rigid grid but a controlled scatter with visible structure: denser lanes and sheets where the swarm has thickened against an expected attack vector, thinner veils elsewhere. Individually near-invisible, together they read as a glittering haze of tiny glints and green status-points, like dust made of intent. Dozens of faint simultaneous gas-puffs show the swarm continuously repositioning.

THE INTERCEPTION: deep inside the cloud, one incoming enemy missile has just been killed — a small, hard white flash (kinetic impact, drone sacrificing itself by collision — no explosives needed at these speeds) with a spray of glittering fragments and a tumbling missile body shedding pieces. Nearby drones are already flowing to close the gap.

BACKGROUND AND LIGHT: black space, sparse stars, dim red Proxima starlight; the Alpha itself out of frame — the cloud is the subject.

FORBIDDEN: no visible propulsion flames, no laser fire from the drones, no insect or eye-like styling — these are disposable geometric machines; the beauty is the swarm, not the unit.
