ENTITY #16 — THE CORE (Scene 17)

Reference design sheet, wide-panel study: THE CORE — Medusa's birthplace and main body, parked at a supermassive black hole because the accretion disk is the largest free reactor in the region. Structure: a tensegrity web thousands of kilometers across — but every thread is anatomy, not decoration: some filaments are particle-beam power lines carrying harvested accretion energy; others are PELLET MASS-STREAMS — literal cargo, streams of refined feedstock slugs in flight between nodes, glowing faintly with friction heating, like luminous plankton moving along threads. Nodes: foundry clusters and shipyard cradles where drone hulls grow in ranks. Energy harvesters: vast VANTABLACK collector sails visible only as absences — perfect black silhouettes against the blinding gravitationally-lensed light of the accretion disk behind them. Dendritic radiator spurs glow ember-red at the web's shadowed side. No lights of habitation anywhere — only function. Include a scale silhouette: an entire planet Earth would fit between two adjacent nodes.

Usage note: the glowing "veins" are physical cargo in flight — render as dotted luminous streams, not solid light-lines.
