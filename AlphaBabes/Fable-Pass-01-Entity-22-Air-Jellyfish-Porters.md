ENTITY #22 — THE AIR-JELLYFISH PORTERS (Scene 7)

Reference design sheet: the AIR-JELLYFISH PORTERS — living airships, domesticated the way humans domesticated donkeys: they were already going that way. Body: a translucent dome-bladder, pearl-and-rose iridescence, filled with self-fermented hydrogen; propulsion by rings of small VALVE-JETS around the dome's skirt, puffing measured breaths of gas; steering by trailing tendril-vanes. Beneath the dome: a natural harness-girdle of tough cartilage rings where the cloud-city dwellers hang woven cargo baskets. Sensory: a ring of simple light-sensing eye-spots around the dome's equator — no face, no front, no expression. Behavior panels: cargo-laden convoy drifting between cloud-city platforms; a juvenile bobbing upward with an over-eager valve-puff. They are gentle, slow, and patient — beasts of burden made of sky.
