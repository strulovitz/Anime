## SPECIES #1 — THE ZĬRÁN (Kepler-442b, 1.3g, dim orange star)

CRITICAL: NOTHING about this creature may resemble a human, a primate, or any Earth animal in costume. No human face, no human torso, no two-arms-two-legs body plan.

EVOLUTIONARY LOGIC: evolved on a 1.3-Earth-gravity forest world under a dim orange star. High gravity forbids tall bodies — they are LOW, WIDE and STABLE. Dim light rewarded a skin partnership with photosynthetic algae. Dense misty forests rewarded sound over sight — they speak in music.

BODY PLAN: a low, graceful HEXAPOD, like a living six-limbed mangrove root, about 1.2 meters tall and 1.6 meters long, massing like a large dog but broader. FOUR sturdy walking limbs, column-like, each ending in a wide splayed four-toed grip-pad (evolved for root-climbing). TWO forward manipulator arms, longer and far more slender, boneless and muscular like elephant trunks, each ending in THREE prehensile finger-tendrils that can split apart for delicate work (pouring tea, weaving) or wrap together into one strong grasp. The body is a smooth horizontal teardrop torso that rises at the front into a gentle swan-neck arc topped by the sensory crown.

SKIN: soft, matte, slightly translucent outer skin hosting colonies of SYMBIOTIC ALGAE in dappled patches of olive-green, warm bronze and pale cream. This is real photosynthesis. The patches are also their emotional display: patterns drift slowly across the skin with mood (calm = broad still patches; joy = bright expanding rings; grief = the dapples stream slowly downward like falling leaves). Pattern unique per individual.

SENSORY CROWN (no face!): the neck-arc ends not in a head but in a whorl-crown of FIVE flexible frond-stalks: THREE eye-stalks tipped with glossy dark hemispherical eyes adapted to dim orange light (huge pupil, amber-ring iris), able to look in independent directions or converge forward with attention (convergence = eye contact); TWO broader feather-edged acoustic fronds — their ears — which ripple toward whatever they listen to. Beneath the crown: the VOICE-BELLOWS, a soft pleated resonating organ like a living accordion of skin, producing flute-like chords, three notes at once. Small simple feeding mouth underneath the torso, discreet, rarely seen.

CLOTHING: no tailored clothes — a single elegant woven MANTLE of moss-fiber and pale silk draped across the torso's back like a ceremonial saddle-blanket, edge-knotted with braided cord and small polished seed-beads.

GREETING: bow by folding the two front walking limbs and lowering the sensory crown while spreading both manipulator arms wide open, tendrils unfurled and empty — "I hold nothing, I force nothing."

MOOD: serene, wise, gentle, utterly alien yet instantly lovable — a creature you'd trust with your garden and your secrets. Pixar-level appeal through posture, softness and the glowing dappled skin — NOT through any human features.

## SPECIES #2 — THE ONDINES (Gliese 876 d, lightless global ocean)

CRITICAL: no legs, no arms-and-torso, no face, no mermaid clichés, no resemblance to humans OR the Zĭrán. Completely independent evolutionary line.

EVOLUTIONARY LOGIC: evolved in the lightless crushing depths of a global ocean on Gliese 876 d. No sunlight ever reached them — so SOUND became their sight, and they grew their own light. No seafloor legs, no land, no fire: a civilization of swimmers who build by growing coral-glass and speak in sonar and glow.

BODY PLAN: a living bell — the main body is a graceful, muscular MANTLE shaped like an elongated closed tulip of translucent flesh, iridescent teal shading to silver at the edges, smooth and hydrodynamic. Propulsion: a directional JET SIPHON at the mantle's rear pole for speed, plus two long RIBBON-FINS running the mantle's length — for slow, precise, hovering movement.

MANIPULATION: at the mantle's front pole, a CROWN OF TWELVE TENTACLES in two rings — an outer ring of eight long ribbon-like arms with translucent silk-fine trailing edges, and an inner ring of four short fine PRECISION TENDRILS that split at their tips into dozens of hair-thin feelers.

THE SONAR DOME (their "face" that is not a face): a smooth, beautiful acoustic MELON — a translucent dome of pale silver flesh. This is how they SEE: they paint the world in sound. When an Ondine "looks" at you, the dome inclines toward you and you feel a gentle thrum. Below the dome, a ring of FOUR SMALL EYES — simple, dark, pupil-less spheres.

LIGHT — their soul: constellations of PHOTOPHORES (bioluminescent light-organs) in elegant lines along the mantle, ribbon-fins and arms — pinpoints of cool teal, silver-white and warm gold. Sonar carries the WORDS, light carries the FEELING: calm = slow silver waves; joy = quick golden sparkles; grief = all lights dimming to a single slow pulse.

TANK-SUIT (for dry land): a transparent, water-filled acrylic vessel shaped to the Ondine's bell body, mounted on a graceful four-legged walking exo-frame of pale alloy; the tentacle crown reaches into flexible liquid-filled sleeve-gauntlets to manipulate the dry world; small silver bubbles rise inside; the sonar dome glows softly near the top like a lantern in a lighthouse.

MOOD: grace, welcome and song — a being of light in the dark, utterly alien and instantly beautiful. Pixar-level appeal through flow, glow and gentle inclination — NEVER through human features.

## SPECIES #3 — THE TITANITES (3g world)

CRITICAL: no bipedal stance, no human torso or face, no resemblance to humans, the Zĭrán, or the Ondines. An entirely independent evolutionary line from a brutal high-gravity world.

EVOLUTIONARY LOGIC: evolved on a world with THREE TIMES Earth's gravity. There, a fall of half a meter kills; nothing tall, thin or fragile survives. Evolution answered with width, weight, armor and patience — and with a voice made of thunder.

BODY PLAN: a living fortress — a low, broad, dome-backed body carried on EIGHT massive pillar-legs arranged in two rows of four, each leg short, thick as a tree trunk, ending in a wide cushioned foot-pad with anchoring claws; the gait is a slow, unstoppable, earthquake-steady ripple (four feet always planted). At the front, the dome tapers into a blunt armored PROW — not a raised head; nothing on this world lifts its head. Folded beneath the prow: TWO heavy TOOL-LIMBS, thick and elbowed like a mantis built by a blacksmith, each ending in a three-pronged gripper — and nested inside each gripper, a bundle of six short fine feeler-digits for delicate work.

ARMOR: the entire back and flanks are covered in BIOMINERALIZED PLATES — the creature grows living rock: overlapping slabs of slate-grey and iron-blue mineral, hexagonal-tending, weathered and lichen-flecked, with visible growth rings on each plate. Old Titanites are literally craggier.

SENSES: beneath a jutting mineral BROW-SHELF at the prow sit FOUR EYES in a low horizontal row — small relative to the body but deeply expressive: dark amber, wet, patient, heavy-lidded, with a slow deliberate blink that is their species' smile. No visible nose or outer ears: they hear through their legs — seismic receptors in the foot-pads.

VOICE: a great RESONANCE CHAMBER inside the dome-back — subsonic rumble-language that listeners feel in the chest before they hear it; when a Titanite speaks, dust dances on the floor.

CULTURE: Titanites wear no clothes — instead their plates are ENGRAVED: fine chiseled scrollwork and honor-marks carved into their own living rock by clan artisans, inlaid with threads of meteor-metal. Salute: one mighty gripper pressed flat over the chest-plate, all four eyes closed, head lowered — strength choosing reverence.

MOOD: immovable gentleness — a mountain that learned kindness. Pixar-level appeal through the patient amber eyes, the slow blink and the careful way something so huge moves around something so small — NEVER through human features.

## SPECIES #4 — THE AERIANS (low-g, dense atmosphere sky world)

CRITICAL: this is NOT a bird, NOT an angel, NOT a bat, NOT a winged human. No beaked bird-face clichés, no feathered humanoid. An entirely independent evolutionary line.

EVOLUTIONARY LOGIC: evolved on a low-gravity world wrapped in a deep, dense, nutrient-rich atmosphere — a planet of eternal cloud oceans, floating aerial plankton, and only rare rocky spires. Evolution never invented comfortable ground: it invented permanent flight.

BODY PLAN: a living glider — the core is a slender aerodynamic KEEL-BODY like the fuselage of a sailplane grown from bone and pearl-grey hide, tapering to a long triple-vaned steering TAIL. Two immense MEMBRANE WINGS, each stretched across THREE elongated spar-fingers, the membrane itself translucent at the trailing edge, catching light like oiled silk. At mid-wing, a small hardened WRIST-HOOK — on the rare ground, they fold their wings into tall narrow tents and STILT-WALK on these hooks with eerie, courteous grace. Tucked beneath the chest keel: TWO small, fine FORE-ARMS with four slender fingers each — delicate precision hands; in flight they fold flush and invisible.

PLUME-SCALES: the leading edges of the wings, the keel-spine and the crest carry overlapping PLUME-SCALES — narrow leaf-shaped blades with microscopic structural ridges producing intense IRIDESCENCE — teal, violet, gold, shifting with viewing angle. Aerians angle these plumes to flash color-patterns — the visual half of their language and their heraldry. Calm sheen, greeting-flash, mourning-grey (plumes flattened).

HEAD & SENSES: a slender lance-shaped head with NO beak — instead a soft-edged, slightly flared intake mouth evolved for sieving aerial plankton, framed by fine baleen-like fringe combs. FOUR EYES: two large forward flight-eyes, deep violet with horizontal pupils, and two small lateral eyes. No outer ears; instead, WIND-LINES — rows of fine pressure-sensitive filaments tracing elegant curves along the head and wing roots.

VOICE: an internal flute-organ produces layered whistling harmonics — melodious, breathy, chord-like — always sounding faintly like weather.

CULTURE: a single woven SASH of spider-silk-fine cord crossing the keel; elders wear thin engraved rings of pale alloy on the wing-spar fingers, one ring per completed great-migration.

MOOD: serene mastery of an element we drown in — patrician grace, watchful courtesy, a creature that regards gravity as a foreign custom it politely tolerates. Pixar-level appeal through the tilt of the lance-head, the shimmer-language of the plumes and the careful stilt-walking dignity — NEVER through human or bird clichés.

## SPECIES #5 — THE DELPHINIDAE (warm shallow seas, reef world)

CRITICAL: despite the name, absolutely NOT dolphin-shaped — no dolphin snout, no whale tail, no Earth-cetacean anatomy, no mermaids, no resemblance to the deep-sea Ondines. Independent evolutionary line.

EVOLUTIONARY LOGIC: evolved in warm, brilliantly SUNLIT shallow seas of a tide-swept reef world — endless light, endless color, ferocious tides. Where the abyss breeds sound and glow, the shallows breed VISION and PATTERN.

BODY PLAN: an underwater flyer — a smooth, supple, streamlined body propelled NOT by a tail fluke but by TWO GREAT PECTORAL WING-FINS spanning wider than the body is long, flapping in slow powerful strokes. At mid-wing, a stiffened wrist-edge on which the animal can PROP AND CRAWL when hauled out on land — an awkward, endearing seal-meets-oarsman shuffle. The rear body tapers to a short triple-vane steering rudder — decisively not a cetacean tail. TWO PAIRED SNORKEL-SIPHONS along the spine — air-breathers.

MANIPULATION: no arms — instead, framing the mouth, FOUR FEEDING-HANDS: short, strong, prehensile palps, each splitting into five nimble finger-tendrils. They weave, build and write with the face.

SKIN — their glory: densely packed CHROMATOPHORES make the entire body a living display: base coloration of warm coral-rose and sand-gold with lagoon-green counter-shading, across which PATTERNS MOVE — their visual speech: rippling bands for names, blooming rosettes for joy, sharp chevrons for warning, and slow spreading pale-grey wash for grief. Malnourished individuals show dulled, low-contrast skin that revives to brilliance with health.

HEAD & SENSES: a broad, gentle, blunt head with NO protruding snout. FOUR EYES in a shallow arc — two large main eyes with W-shaped pupils (superb color vision) and two small upward sky-eyes. A smooth sensory brow of pressure-reading pores. The mouth beneath the feeding-hands is small and neat.

VOICE: modest — soft percussive clicks and low warbles from the siphons, used only as attention-getters; the real language is always the skin.

TANK-SUIT (for the council and dry-land scenes): a horizontal transparent water-filled vessel fitted to the swimming body, carried on a six-wheeled utility frame; the four feeding-hands reach into fluid-filled instrument-sleeves at the front; a pattern-translation screen mounted on the tank's brow.

MOOD: warmth, family, resilience — a species that speaks in rainbows. Pixar-level appeal through the juveniles' clumsy wing-crawl, the parents' sheltering wing-fins and the living paint-box skin — NEVER through human features.

EVOLUTIONARY LOGIC: evolved on a harsh, dry, open steppe-world under a fierce bright star — endless plains with no cover, where death approaches from any direction. Evolution's answer was TRILATERAL SYMMETRY: no blind side.

BODY PLAN: a vertical column-torso of lean hard muscle rising from a central hip-ring, carried on THREE long digitigrade legs spaced evenly around it. From a shoulder-ring at the torso's top spring THREE ARMS, also 120 degrees apart, wiry and long, each ending in a four-digit hand (two fingers opposed by two thumbs). Commander Zev's right arm is a cybernetic prosthetic: gunmetal and scorched brass, mismatched replacement plates, field-repair weld scars.

HEAD & SENSES: the torso tapers into a sensor CROWN — a smooth dome ringed by THREE large eyes spaced 120 degrees apart, narrow, heavy-lidded, ember-orange with slit pupils. Above them, a low crest of heat-dissipating fin-ridges. Between the eyes, three vertical BREATHING VENTS with flexible lips — these are also his voice: dry, rasping DUAL-TONE. The small feeding mouth is low on the torso, private, kept covered.

HIDE & EMOTION: hide like sun-cracked basalt leather in dusty tan and iron-grey. Skarn show emotion through vents and crest: calm = slow vent-rhythm; anger = crest flushing dark red; grief = all three eyes closing in sequence, one after another.

MOOD: hard-bitten honor — a soldier built by a merciless world and a worse empire. Pixar-level appeal through the tired ember eyes and the body language — NEVER through human features.
