# Technical Entity Reference Image — Template Examples (for Claude Fable)

These are the two example entity prompts Nir picked to show Fable the FIXED TEMPLATE we use
for purely technical/machine reference images (no aliens, no humans — just hard engineering).
Fable can reformat his new reference prompts (e.g. the tidal hydraulic tower, water-column
cannons, sphere-in-sphere capsules) to match this style.

## EXAMPLE 1 — Entity #48: Alpha Control Room (v2-FIXED)

Pixar/Disney 3D animation style, cinematic feature-film quality render. Warm, charming, emotionally expressive characters with the signature polished "Pixar finish" — but NOT a toy world: realistic proportions, real physical materials (brushed copper, scuffed metal, woven fabric, ice, dust), physically accurate lighting with soft global illumination, shallow cinematic depth of field. Hard science fiction: every technical detail scientifically plausible, no fantasy glow, no magic. Original design — must not resemble any existing anime or movie franchise. 16:9 widescreen, ultra-detailed, movie-still composition.

SUBJECT: Control room inside one submarine-shaped hull of the interstellar ship Alpha — design language: modern nuclear-submarine control room combined with spacecraft glass cockpit, year ~2050. NO windows, NO portholes, NO steering wheel. No people.

CAMERA: wide interior shot from the forward end of the compartment looking aft, slight low angle, 24 mm lens equivalent.

ROOM GEOMETRY: horizontal cylinder interior, 4 m diameter, 7 m long, flat deck floor. Structure: bare copper pressure-hull ribs (semicircular, 20 cm deep, dull reddish-brown metal, machine-finished, NOT polished decorative) every 60 cm, with matte white acoustic panels between them. All cable runs in white sheathing, clamped in straight dressed lines, small e-paper label tags at intervals.

MAIN FEATURE (aft bulkhead, facing camera): a seamless curved wall-sized microLED display, 4 m wide, 2 m tall, wrapping 120 degrees. Content: a live sensor-fusion starfield (accurate stars on black) overlaid with thin clean vector graphics in cyan and white: a curved trajectory line, a dotted line of pre-positioned fuel pellets with numeric intercept timers, two small diamond-shaped debris track markers in yellow, and a small circled star labeled by a thin ring (destination). Graphics style: flight-instrument minimalism, no decorative holograms.

SEATING: exactly THREE crash couches, all FACING the display wall (i.e., seats face aft; occupants' backs point toward direction of flight). Couches: white composite shell, dark grey padding, visible six-point harness straps neatly clipped, mounted on shock-absorber struts. Each couch has a console on an articulated swing arm: a slim touchscreen plus a row of physical guarded switches. Under each seat: a stowage bay with a folded dark-grey G-suit and a compact rebreather unit visible behind a clear latch door.

CENTER: a round plot table, 1 m diameter, projecting a crisp monochrome cyan wireframe hologram 40 cm tall: six parallel submarine-shaped hulls arranged in a ring around a central empty tube, one hull highlighted brighter.

DECK: recessed LED arrow strips running along the floor toward the exit hatch, currently unlit (dark grey). AFT WALL, right side: an open circular one-person escape chamber hatch, 80 cm diameter, 15 cm thick door with dogged latches, padded orange interior with harness visible.

DETAILS: one sealed metal drink mug docked in a spring holder on a console rail; a small wall panel showing a six-digit day counter.
LIGHTING: cool blue-white glow from the display wall as key light; dim red night-lighting strips along the ceiling; no other light sources.
16:9, photorealistic materials in Pixar 3D feature style.

## EXAMPLE 2 — Entity #98: Bussard Ramjet Dish

Pixar/Disney 3D animation style, cinematic feature-film quality render. Warm, charming, emotionally expressive characters with the signature polished "Pixar finish" — but NOT a toy world: realistic proportions, real physical materials (brushed copper, scuffed metal, woven fabric, ice, dust), physically accurate lighting with soft global illumination, shallow cinematic depth of field. Hard science fiction: every technical detail scientifically plausible, no fantasy glow, no magic. Original design — must not resemble any existing anime or movie franchise. 16:9 widescreen, ultra-detailed, movie-still composition.

SUBJECT: the BUSSARD RAMJET DISH at the bow of the interstellar mothership Alpha — an enormous shallow geodesic collection dish that drinks hydrogen from the void, doubles as a great telescope, and hides teeth. Single full-frame reference image: three-quarter front view from slightly off-axis, the dish filling most of the frame, the ship's six copper submarine hulls receding behind its rim into darkness. Deep space background, sparse stars.

THE DISH — engineering truth: an immense, shallow, concave geodesic dish many times wider than the ship's body, assembled from HUNDREDS of flat triangular GRAPHENE panels — near-black, whisper-thin, with a faint dark iridescent sheen — each triangle framed by slender CARBON-NANOTUBE edge struts forming a visible, perfectly regular geodesic lattice across the whole surface. The panels catch the dim COAL-RED light of Proxima Centauri at a raking angle, so the dish reads as a vast dark honeycomb with red-glinting facet edges. At the dish's exact center: the dark open throat where collected hydrogen funnels into the ship's hollow central cylinder (the solenoid that runs the vessel's whole length).

THE TELESCOPE: suspended above the dish's center on three slender nanotube tripod booms, a small GOLD-PLATED secondary mirror — brilliant warm gold, the single brightest object in the frame, mirror-smooth, throwing a faint golden reflection down onto the dark panels beneath it (the same optical architecture as a great space telescope: the dish is also the ship's eye).

THE HIDDEN TEETH: among the triangular panels nearest the central throat, a few triangles are subtly OUTLINED — flush hatch seams with tiny recessed latches, barely distinguishable from their neighbors: closed missile ports, visible only to a careful observer. Do not open them.

SCALE AND CONTEXT: behind the dish's rim, the ship's six dull-copper submarine hulls and their lumpy white ice-bag quilting recede out of focus. A faint human silhouette stands on a maintenance gantry at the dish's rim, microscopic against the structure.

FORBIDDEN: no glowing energy field, no visible "scoop beam," no sci-fi forcefield — the dish is dark honest matter; the only lights are red starlight, gold mirror, and faint hull glints.
