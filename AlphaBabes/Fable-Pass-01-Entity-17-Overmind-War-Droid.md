ENTITY #17 — THE OVERMIND + ITS WAR-DROID (Scene 16)

Reference design sheet, two subjects. SUBJECT 1 — THE OVERMIND CORE CHAMBER: a vast spherical hall lined with concentric rings of slender open CRYOSTAT TOWERS — crystalline-sheathed quantum computing columns, frost-rimed at their bases; between them, a woven mesh of cyan light: the photonic interconnect lasers threading column to column — the visible nervous system. Central hovering core ringed like a mechanical iris. DEATH-STATE panel: interconnect lasers going dark ring by ring, and the chamber FROSTING OVER — white coolant boil-off pluming from failing cryogenics; a machine's corpse turns cold-white, not dramatic-red. SUBJECT 2 — THE WAR-DROID: a four-meter bipedal security machine, gunmetal, heavy-shouldered — bipedal for one honest engineering reason: it was built to storm corridors, airlocks and stairways built by bipeds; its shape is a negative image of its victims' architecture. Panels: standing pose, and defeated pose — limbs tangled hopelessly in weighted steel nets, sparks bursting from joints as its master-signal dies (legs are a liability physics never forgives).

Usage note: Overmind's color is CYAN — never red; it is Medusa's puppet, not Medusa, and the palette must say so.
