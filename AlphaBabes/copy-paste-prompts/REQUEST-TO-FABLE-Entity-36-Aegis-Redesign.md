## Where Mirror Drones Appear In The Story (from the 5-season story arc)

The mirror drones are the Alliance's CORE tactical invention — they ARE "Project Zet" (3D laser chess):

1. **Invention:** Tink created them as "experimental prototypes for a 3D laser-combat system." First deployed in Episode 2 — a PAIR of them flanked a Dominion patrol ship, caught its laser blasts, reflected one beam into the enemy's own flank, and redirected another harmlessly into space. The stunned enemy commander hesitated, and the Alpha-Babes won.

2. **Project Zet Named:** Commander Zev's tactical advisors were so impressed they dubbed the strategy "Project Zet" — seeing its potential in 3D space warfare where resources are limited.

3. **Outpost Delta Battle:** Tink orchestrated a "web" of mirror drones around an asteroid base. Two drones reflected an enemy cruiser's laser at 90-degree angles — the beam split and slashed into one of the cruiser's OWN escort carriers. The formation scattered in panic.

4. **Decoy Drones:** Tink anticipated the enemy would try to shoot down the mirrors. She outfitted some units as DECOYS — same reflective surfaces, but they intentionally draw enemy fire and sacrifice themselves while the crucial mirrors remain positioned.

5. **Zenith Shipyard (full-scale):** Mariner and Lyra fed coordinates to DOZENS of mirror drones forming an "intricate three-dimensional pattern" around the enemy. Ace's flagship laser was fired into a cluster of mirror drones at an oblique angle — the beam pinballed between FOUR drones in a trapezoidal formation and struck a destroyer from its vulnerable rear. Simultaneously, another wing of drones redirected a cruiser's laser in a "sweeping arc" that scythed through automated gun satellites.

6. **Perseus Gambit (final battle):** The mirror drones were assembled into the "Aegis Array" — a coordinated swarm designed to reflect Medusa's own primary weapon back at it. In the final battle, "dozens of ships glinting in starlight, against one massive silhouette" — the mirror drones moved into place "like pieces of a puzzle."

7. **Standardized:** By Season 4, Tink helps standardize a mirror drone squadron in EVERY fleet — Project Zet baked into every battle group. Simulations show that having a few laser-redirecting units dramatically increases hit probability against AI ships.
