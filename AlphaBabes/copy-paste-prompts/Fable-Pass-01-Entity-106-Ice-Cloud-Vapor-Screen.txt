SUBJECT: the ICE-CLOUD VAPOR SCREEN — a deliberate shimmering veil of high-altitude ice vapor deployed over Kepler-442b, an artificial aurora that eats falling meteors. Single full-frame reference image: night sky view from high altitude, the veil stretching across the frame with meteor fragments dying against it.

THE SCREEN — hard-science defense: a vast, deliberately seeded sheet of microscopic ICE CRYSTALS spread through the high atmosphere — a manufactured noctilucent cloud, kilometers wide, paper-thin from the side. Incoming meteor fragments, fragile and fast, hit this sudden wall of dense vapor and ABLATE — boiling away harmlessly in the upper air before they can reach the city below. No force field, no energy barrier: just water, altitude and physics.

THE LOOK: the veil hangs as luminous rippled CURTAINS and streamers, pearly silver-blue, sparkling like an artificial aurora — its light honest: ice crystals scattering starlight, the light of TWO SMALL SILVERY MOONS, and the glow of the burning fragments themselves. Fine wave-structure and cellular ripples run through the sheet (real noctilucent-cloud morphology); its edges thin into gauze and vanish.

THE INTERCEPTIONS — physics as drama: several blazing METEOR TRAILS streak steeply down from the upper frame — brilliant white-orange lines with glowing smoke — and where each meets the veil it FLARES, fattens and dies: a bright terminal bloom, a shower of sparks fading to embers, a punched glowing hole in the curtain slowly closing. One large fragment mid-flare is the focal point. Below the veil: the safe indigo darkness of the sleeping world, no ground detail.

MOOD: a shield made of weather — silent, luminous, merciful; the night the sky was caught.
