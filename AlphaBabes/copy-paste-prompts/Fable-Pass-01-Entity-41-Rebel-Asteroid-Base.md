# Entity #41 — Great Hall of the Hidden Rebel Base

## What the image shows
The empty Great Hall inside the hollowed asteroid, the night before the first
Council of Species convenes. A gold holographic star map rotates above a
central rock-and-copper holo-table, casting warm light across raw mining-scarred
stone walls, copper girders, and jury-rigged fusion lamps.

## Design notes
- **Zero reference images required** — prompt is fully self-contained.
- All species names and anatomical trigger words ("eight-legged", "winged",
  "hexapod") removed from the image prompt to prevent creature hallucination.
- Anatomy is implied only through *wear marks*: leg depressions in stone,
  scratch grooves on the perch — the room remembers its occupants.
- Emphatic multi-clause "empty room" instruction closes the prompt.

## Mood
Improvised, warm, defiant — a cave that decided to become a parliament.
