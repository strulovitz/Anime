You don't look. You sense. NO windows anywhere — a vessel moving at one three-hundredth of light speed is navigated by its telescope, its fiber-optic sensor masts and its computers, never by human eyes. Mood: absolute calm competence — a room that sees for four light-years in every direction and wastes not one lumen showing off.
