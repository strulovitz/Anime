Pixar/Disney 3D animation style, cinematic feature-film quality render. Hard science fiction: every technical detail scientifically plausible, no fantasy glow, no magic. Original design — must not resemble any existing anime or movie franchise. 16:9 widescreen, ultra-detailed, movie-still composition.

Reference design sheet, two-panel study: MEDUSA'S PROJECTION — how a mind of pure calculation presents itself: not a face, not an avatar, but its live working plan. PANEL 1, ACTIVE STATE: a floor-to-ceiling volumetric map of the entire galaxy rendered in cold deep-red data-light — a hundred billion stars in a slow spiral; across it, Medusa's claim crawling in real time: red lattice-borders advancing, fleet-icons in motion, living worlds being re-labeled with feedstock glyphs. Woven into the map: a thousand grey extinguished markers at the sites where machine minds once rose and vanished — the graveyard statistics of the Wager. A single floating probability expression hangs beside them. PANEL 2, FROZEN STATE: the same map with every border and every icon stopped mid-crawl — the visible moment when all computation is redirected to checking one human argument; slightly dimmer, absolutely still. Human figure silhouette included for scale: the map towers floor-to-ceiling around a single small standing woman.
