Single full-frame interior masterpiece: the COUNCIL HALL OF THE COALITION — the one room in the galaxy built for every body-plan at once, in session recess, nearly empty, telling its story through its furniture. The space: a great circular chamber carved into the rebel asteroid base's heart, its dome ceiling left as raw polished asteroid stone threaded with glittering metal veins, and at its apex a round skylight of thick glass showing the stars the coalition sails. The floor plan is the diplomacy: a ring of speaking stations around a central holo-well, and NO TWO STATIONS ALIKE — each built by its own species for its own body and gifted to the hall: the TITANITE station a massive iron bench, floor visibly reinforced beneath it, heat-lamp glowing above; the ONDINE station a magnificent flooded glass TANK with sculpted coral seating, connected to the floor by a wet ramp, its water casting ripple-light on the ceiling; the AERIAN station no chair at all — a beautiful bone-lattice PERCH mounted high on the wall with its own wind from a soft ventilator; the human station modest and warm — a plain wooden chair with a cushion, and on its small side table, unmistakably, a steaming mug; other stations stranger still — a resonant crystal alcove, a low basin of fine sand, a frame for a body that hangs. At center: the holo-well projecting a slow gold map of coalition space, its light touching every empty seat equally. Details rewarding a close look: translation pylons between stations, each engraved in many scripts; a signed founding charter displayed under glass, marked in ink, claw-print, sonar-etch and dissolved pigment; scuff marks showing where stations were dragged CLOSER together over the years. Lighting: warm holo-gold key, ripple-light from the tank, starlight from above. Mood: the opposite of the throne room — power as a circle, and every chair a compromise lovingly made.
