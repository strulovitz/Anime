## What These Drones Actually Do In Battle

This is NOT generic sci-fi combat. In the Alpha Babes universe, space battles operate on **3D laser chess logic** (called "Project Zet"). Here's how it works:

### Core Principles:
- Lasers travel in perfectly straight lines. No curving, no tracking.
- **Position matters.** **Angles matter.** The entire battle is geometry.
- Mirror drones are deployed in formations to **reflect laser beams at oblique angles**, redirecting them toward enemy targets from unexpected directions.
- A single laser beam can **pinball between multiple mirror drones** (e.g. 4 drones in a trapezoidal formation), emerging to strike a target from its vulnerable rear.
- Enemies can be forced to shoot themselves — their own laser is reflected back at them.
- Formations can make lasers **split and sweep** — a beam hits one mirror, splits, and scythes through a whole formation.
- Decoy mirror drones are sacrificed to draw enemy fire while the real mirrors stay positioned.
- Anti-missile drone clouds: dozens of mini-drones disperse into a floating cloud; a scanning laser detonates the cloud in the path of incoming missiles.
- The enemy AI (Overmind, later Medusa) treats its own units as **expendable chess pawns** — pure geometry, zero empathy.
- The visual style is meant to feel like **chess rather than chaotic warfare** — clear readable formations, strategic elegance.

### What This Means For Drone Design:

The drones — both individually and as a swarm — are the PIECES in this 3D chess game. They need:

1. **Reflective/mirrored surfaces** — they ARE the mirrors in "mirror drone." Their hull must be able to reflect and redirect lasers at precise angles.
2. **Clean geometric shapes** — readable formations require readable individual units. The audience needs to see the beam path bounce from drone to drone and understand the geometry.
3. **Coordinated swarm behavior** — they move in formations (trapezoidal, web, school-of-predators), not random chaos.
4. **Expendable-by-design** — the AI treats them as disposable chess pieces. They should look mass-produced, practical, efficient, not precious.
