EVOLUTIONARY LOGIC (drives every detail): the Zĭrán evolved on a 1.3-Earth-gravity forest world under a dim orange star. High gravity forbids tall bodies — they are LOW, WIDE and STABLE. Dim light rewarded a skin partnership with photosynthetic algae. Dense misty forests rewarded sound over sight — they speak in music.

BODY PLAN: a low, graceful HEXAPOD, like a living six-limbed mangrove root, about 1.2 meters tall and 1.6 meters long, massing like a large dog but broader. FOUR sturdy walking limbs, column-like, each ending in a wide splayed four-toed grip-pad (evolved for root-climbing) — plus TWO forward manipulator arms, longer and far more slender, boneless and muscular like elephant trunks, each ending in THREE prehensile finger-tendrils that can split apart for delicate work (pouring tea, weaving) or wrap together into one strong grasp. The body is a smooth horizontal teardrop torso that rises at the front into a gentle swan-neck arc topped by the sensory crown — giving them a posture like a patient gardener forever leaning over a seedling.

SKIN — their glory: soft, matte, slightly translucent outer skin hosting colonies of SYMBIOTIC ALGAE in dappled patches of olive-green, warm bronze and pale cream — exactly like sun-shadow through leaves. This is real photosynthesis: they part-feed on their star's light. The patches are also their emotional display: patterns drift slowly across the skin with mood (calm = broad still patches; joy = bright expanding rings; grief or awe = the dapples stream slowly downward like falling leaves). Pattern unique per individual, like fingerprints.

Beneath the crown, on the "chest" of the neck-arc: the VOICE-BELLOWS, a soft pleated resonating organ like a living accordion of skin, which produces their language — flute-like chords, three notes at once, visible as a gentle pulsing of the pleats when they speak. There is a small simple feeding mouth underneath the torso, discreet, rarely seen.
