SUBJECT: the MAIN EMERALD LASER CANNON of a Dominion war cruiser — the enemy's terrible primary weapon, the beam that will one day be turned against its own ship. Single full-frame reference image: three-quarter view of the cannon mount at the moment of firing, seated on the cruiser's angular dark hull, deep space background.

THE HOST SHIP — enemy aesthetics: the visible hull around the mount is the Dominion's language of menace — hard angular facets of near-black armor plating, knife-edge chines, recessed panel lines, and sparse COLD BLUE running lights tracing the geometry. Everything about it is precise, mass-produced, merciless — the opposite of the rebels' warm riveted copper.

THE CANNON — engineering truth: a massive two-axis turret the size of a house on a faceted ring-bearing collar. The weapon itself is not a gun barrel but a LASER OPTICAL TRAIN — a thick angular housing ending in a wide focusing aperture with a visible multi-lens assembly, its front element glowing green with leaked light. Because lasers live and die by heat: prominent radiator vanes flare from the housing's flanks glowing faint dull orange under fire, and armored coolant trunks run back into the hull. A boxy cold-eyed targeting sensor cluster rides the mount. Scorch-darkening around the aperture from long use.

THE BEAM — physically honest: a single, ruler-straight, brilliant EMERALD-GREEN beam leaving the aperture and crossing the entire frame, visible because the battlefield is fogged with vaporized debris and drifting dust scattering the light (in clean vacuum it would be invisible — the medium reveals it). Thin and absolute — no wobble, no pulses, no "bolts." An intense green bloom at the aperture; far off where the beam exits frame, a small incandescent flare of a target beginning to vaporize.

LIGHTING: the green beam is the dominant light, throwing hard emerald rim-light along the black faceted armor and clashing with the cold blue running lights — poison-green against ice-blue, the enemy's palette.

FORBIDDEN: no copper, no rivets, no warm light anywhere — this is the enemy's machine; no laser bolts or pulses, no recoil, no muzzle flash — a laser is a straight silent line of light.
