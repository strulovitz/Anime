# Entity 22-WILD — Air-Jellyfish Porters (Natural Wild State)

Same species as Entity 22, but in their natural untamed form — no domestication, no ropes, no baskets, no cargo. Just the creature as evolution made it, drifting freely through the cloud layers of their home world.

**Anatomy (unchanged):** Translucent pearl-and-rose dome-bladder filled with self-fermented hydrogen, valve-jet propulsion rings, trailing tendril-vanes for steering, cartilage rings at base, light-sensing eye-spots around equator.

**Key difference from Entity 22:** No harness, no baskets, no ropes, no cargo. The cartilage rings are bare — simply part of their anatomy. These are wild, free creatures.

**Used in:** Entity #38 (Ross 128b Ocean) — as the reference for air-jellyfish seen underwater in their natural form.
