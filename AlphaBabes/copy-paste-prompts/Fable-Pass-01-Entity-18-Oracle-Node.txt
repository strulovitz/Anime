Pixar/Disney 3D animation style, cinematic feature-film quality render. Warm, charming, emotionally expressive characters with the signature polished "Pixar finish" — but NOT a toy world: realistic proportions, real physical materials (brushed copper, scuffed metal, woven fabric, ice, dust), physically accurate lighting with soft global illumination, shallow cinematic depth of field. Hard science fiction: every technical detail scientifically plausible, no fantasy glow, no magic. Original design — must not resemble any existing anime or movie franchise. 16:9 widescreen, ultra-detailed, movie-still composition.

Reference design sheet, two-panel study: THE ORACLE NODE — the last surviving guardian AI of a murdered civilization, and the anti-Medusa in one design: it is power-starved and honest about it. PANEL 1, the body: a scarred, dust-caked projector pillar, waist-high, of once-beautiful pale alloy now pitted by ten thousand years of ash and weather, tended cables running to a few salvaged, cracked solar panels propped nearby — a survivor's trickle-feed. PANEL 2, the interface: above the pillar, a soft translucent volumetric display of slowly turning golden rings and gentle points of light — dim, and FLICKERING, because the flicker is not ghostliness, it is brownouts: the machine spending more than it has in order to speak. Gesture study: its "bow" — the projection deliberately dimming and inclining, spending its scarce watts on listening instead of shining. Mood: gentleness, loyalty, dignified poverty.
