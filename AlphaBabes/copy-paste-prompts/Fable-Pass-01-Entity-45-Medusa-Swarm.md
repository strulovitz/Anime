# Entity #45 — Medusa Swarm (and the physics of the board)

Hundreds of identical Obol Mk.II units. No unique members; identity = formation.

## Hard constraints that create the chess rules (not lore — physics)
1. LEG LENGTH: diffraction caps each beam leg at ~600 km (spot outgrows
   the mirror). Refocus faces buy back margin; relay chains have max length.
2. LEGAL ANGLES: coatings accept 10°–55° incidence only. Grazing and
   near-normal reflections are illegal moves (drone self-destructs thermally).
3. TEMPO: repositions take 10s of seconds; beams resolve instantly.
   The battle is genuinely turn-based.
4. BEAM VISIBILITY: lasers are invisible in vacuum. Beams appear only
   crossing thruster plumes and debris vapor — the geometry reveals itself
   along the maneuvers. Crews see the full board on tactical plots.

## Formation vocabulary
- Relay Trapezoid (4 units, rear-aspect strike) · Return Web (enemy fire
  reflected to source) · Splitter Fan (beamsplitter-coated unit divides one
  beam) · Sacrifice Screen (decoys flare mirrors under fire) · Vapor Wall
  (micro-drone cloud laser-detonated in a missile's path — kinetic kill by
  debris at closing velocity).

## Both sides field these
- Medusa: bare metal, unlit, unrecoverable — spent like coins.
- Alliance "Aegis": same optics, white-ringed, strobed, refuelable, re-flown.
- Screen shorthand: gray hexagons vs white-ringed hexagons.
