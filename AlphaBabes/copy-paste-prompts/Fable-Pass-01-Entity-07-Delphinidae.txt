Pixar/Disney 3D animation style, cinematic feature-film quality render. Hard science fiction: every technical detail scientifically plausible, no fantasy glow, no magic. Original design — must not resemble any existing anime or movie franchise. 16:9 widescreen, ultra-detailed, movie-still composition.

SPECIES REFERENCE SHEET, clean neutral pale aqua-grey studio background (as if in bright clear water), soft even lighting, crisp focus everywhere — master visual reference for the DELPHINIDAE, a fully NON-HUMANOID aquatic alien species. CRITICAL: despite the name, absolutely NOT dolphin-shaped — no dolphin snout, no whale tail, no Earth-cetacean anatomy, no mermaids, and no resemblance to the deep-sea Ondines. An entirely independent evolutionary line from a different world.

SHOW: (1) a FAMILY GROUP — two adults swimming in three-quarter view with two juveniles between them, one juvenile hauled out resting on a rock ledge to demonstrate their amphibious crawl, (2) one adult in clean side profile, (3) a close-up detail panel of the head: eyes, feeding-hands and a demonstration strip of skin color-language, (4) a vignette of their DIPLOMAT in a water-filled walking tank-suit for dry-land council scenes. Include a faint human silhouette for scale: adults about two meters long.

EVOLUTIONARY LOGIC (drives every detail): the Delphinidae evolved in the warm, brilliantly SUNLIT shallow seas of a tide-swept reef world — endless light, endless color, ferocious tides. Where the abyss breeds sound and glow, the shallows breed VISION and PATTERN: these are creatures whose very skin is a language, who fly through bright water on wing-fins, and who learned to crawl onto the reef flats when the great tides withdrew.

BODY PLAN: an underwater flyer — a smooth, supple, streamlined body like a living shuttle of muscle, propelled NOT by a tail fluke but by TWO GREAT PECTORAL WING-FINS spanning wider than the body is long, flapping in slow powerful strokes exactly like flight (the manta-and-penguin principle, in an alien geometry): the wing-fins are ribbed with flexible cartilage rays and end in a rounded, slightly stiffened wrist-edge on which the animal can PROP AND CRAWL when hauled out on land — an awkward, endearing, seal-meets-oarsman shuffle. The rear body tapers to a short triple-vane steering rudder — decisively not a cetacean tail. Along the spine, a low ridge bearing TWO PAIRED SNORKEL-SIPHONS — they are air-breathers of the surface waters, and the siphons close with soft valves when diving.

MANIPULATION: no arms — instead, framing the mouth, FOUR FEEDING-HANDS: short, strong, prehensile palps, each splitting into five nimble finger-tendrils, evolved for prying shellfish from reef crevices and now the hands of a tool-using civilization: they weave, build and write with the face, which gives conversation with them a peculiar charming intimacy. Show one adult manipulating a small carved shell-instrument.

SKIN — their glory and their language: densely packed CHROMATOPHORES make the entire body a living display: base coloration of warm coral-rose and sand-gold with lagoon-green counter-shading, across which PATTERNS MOVE — their visual speech: rippling bands for names, blooming rosettes for joy, sharp chevrons for warning, and slow spreading pale-grey wash for grief. A calf's first "word" is a color. Show a small annotated strip of four pattern-states. In the rescued-refugee context, note: malnourished individuals show dulled, low-contrast skin that revives to brilliance with health — a visual redemption arc.

HEAD & SENSES: a broad, gentle, blunt head with NO protruding snout: FOUR EYES in a shallow arc — two large main eyes with W-shaped pupils (superb color vision beyond human range) and two small upward sky-eyes watching the surface for diving predators. Between them, a smooth sensory brow of pressure-reading pores. The mouth beneath the feeding-hands is small and neat. Expression lives in the eyes' angle and the skin's color — never in a human smile.

VOICE: modest — soft percussive clicks and low warbles from the siphons, used only as attention-getters; the real language is always the skin. (Their tragedy in dark refugee ship holds: dimmed and colorless, they fall nearly silent.)

TANK-SUIT vignette (for the council and dry-land scenes): a horizontal transparent water-filled vessel fitted to the swimming body, carried on a six-wheeled and-legged Dominion utility frame of battered, mismatched refugee alloy — visibly poorer and more improvised than any polished diplomat's gear; the four feeding-hands reach into fluid-filled instrument-sleeves at the front; the diplomat's skin, healthy again, plays slow dignified bands of gold as it speaks through a pattern-translation screen mounted on the tank's brow.

MOOD OF THE DESIGN: warmth, family, resilience — a species that speaks in rainbows, survived the dark, and got its colors back. Pixar-level appeal through the juveniles' clumsy wing-crawl, the parents' sheltering wing-fins and the living paint-box skin — never through human features.
