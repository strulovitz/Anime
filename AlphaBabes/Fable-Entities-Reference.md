## 1. 🌍 PLANETS & STARS

### Proxima Centauri 🔁
- **Scenes:** 1, 2, 4
- **Description:** Red dwarf star. Dim, coal-red, sullen. Looks ~3× larger than Earth's sun from Proxima b's surface. The Alpha's destination. Hurls stellar flares powerful enough to strip atmospheres bare. Seen as a warm ember at the bow in Scene 1; its red light paints Madie's face through a porthole in Scene 2; sits low on Proxima b's horizon in Scene 4.

### Proxima b 🔁
- **Scenes:** 4, 5
- **Description:** Exoplanet orbiting Proxima Centauri. Rust-colored regolith plain in a twilight zone between scorching day side and frozen night side. Deep-purple sky with shimmering green/violet auroras (flare afterglow). Thin hostile air. Home to lava-tube caves with bioluminescent lichen and fungal mycelium. First exoplanet stepped on by humans.

### Ross 128 b 🔁
- **Scenes:** 6, 7
- **Description:** Exoplanet orbiting an orange-red dwarf star. "A living world." Warm turquoise seas with salmon-colored god-rays. Plants evolve indigo and black to absorb red-dwarf light. Home to a colossal whale-like filter-feeder, translucent fish schools, glass-like silicate reef spires, neon-green seagrass, jellyfish-like creatures. Seen receding as a blue-green marble from the Alpha's bridge in Scene 7.

### Kepler-442b 🔁
- **Scenes:** 8, 9, 10, 11, 12
- **Description:** Exoplanet orbiting a K-type orange dwarf sun. Honeyed amber daylight. Breathable atmosphere. Home of the Zĭrán civilization. Has 1.3× Earth's gravity (the Zĭrán are shorter/sturdier because of it). Features the Tree City, Mt. Jùn (supervolcano), and two small silvery moons. Underwent a comet impact and supervolcano eruption triggered by Medusa.

### Gliese 876 d
- **Scenes:** 18
- **Description:** Exoplanet, a deep global ocean world. Home of the Ondine civilization. Blue-black abyssal depths pierced by bioluminescent cities. Kelp forests at city edges. The first door the crew knocked on to build the coalition.

### Wolf 1061c
- **Scenes:** 19
- **Description:** Dead world — a civilization that fought Medusa and lost. Bruised orange-purple sky, everything dusted in fine grey ash. Ruined plaza with shattered elegant towers, melted defense turrets slumped like candle wax, toppled monument of a graceful alien figure, pale scorched silhouettes burned onto walls where people once stood ("no bodies"). Home of the Oracle Node.

### K2-18b
- **Scenes:** 27
- **Description:** Far-off water world. Destination of the next voyage (the new ship being built in the epilogue). "A new whisper of life."

### Orpheus Nebula
- **Scenes:** 17
- **Description:** At the galaxy's rim. Vast curtains of dark dust and dim ember-red gas. Contains a supermassive black hole at its center. Location of Medusa's Core lair.

### Red dwarf star (battlefield)
- **Scenes:** 21
- **Description:** Dim red dwarf star whose sullen ember light stains the entire battle. Chosen as staging ground deliberately — far from any inhabited world.

### Romanian countryside 🔁
- **Scenes:** 26, (implied in Madie's backstory throughout)
- **Description:** Perfect golden summer afternoon, rolling hills, wildflowers, line of poplars, small traditional house with wooden veranda, family on the porch, table with steaming borscht. Madie's paradise — her reward after death.

### Two silvery moons of Kepler-442b
- **Scenes:** 9, 12
- **Description:** Two small silver moons visible in the night sky of Kepler-442b.

### Milky Way
- **Scenes:** 1, 12
- **Description:** Star-dusted band of the galaxy behind the Alpha in the opening shot. Also visible in the night sky over the farewell ceremony.

### Dominion capital world
- **Scenes:** 16
- **Description:** Homeworld of the Zidrani Dominion. Houses the fortress datacenter tower containing the Overmind's core chamber.

### Dominion space
- **Scenes:** 15
- **Description:** Shown as a rotating golden star map on the holo-table at the rebel base.

## 2. 👽 ALIEN SPECIES & INDIVIDUALS

### Zĭrán 🔁
- **Scenes:** 8, 9, 10, 11, 12
- **Description:** Humanoid aliens native to Kepler-442b. Slightly shorter and sturdier than humans (1.3× Earth gravity). Beautiful dappled skin patterned like leaf-shadow camouflage in olive, bronze, and cream. Gentle wide-set eyes. Dressed in simple elegant robes of woven moss-fiber and pale silk. Bow with serene, unafraid smiles, palms open. A civilization that "built nothing — grew everything." Ended war and waste centuries ago by following one law: flow with nature, and do not force ("wu wei"). No machines, no metal, no wires anywhere.

### Elder Yùn 🔁
- **Scenes:** 9, 10, 12, 19, 20, 24, 27
- **Description:** Aged Zĭrán monk. Dappled leaf-pattern skin gone silvery with age. Kind deep-set eyes. Simple moss-fiber robe. Radiates gentle authority. Performs tea ceremony with slow, perfect, effortless movements. In Scene 10, weeps as his worldview transforms watching science save his people. Boards the Alpha in Scene 12 carrying a small cloth bundle. Chants at the Wolf 1061c vigil. Bows deepest of all at Madie's coronation. Stands in profound reverence at her death. Bows to the sky with the nine sisters in the epilogue ("aged," now present at Madie's memorial). Teaches: "wu wei — actionless action."

### Shaozu 🔁
- **Scenes:** 9, 10, 11, 12, 24, 27
- **Description:** Young Zĭrán monk. Brighter leaf-patterns than Elder Yùn. Eager curious eyes, hungry for the stars. Carries a stack of scroll-books. In Scene 9: kneels at Yùn's side, leaning forward with hunger for the stars. In Scene 10: strains to ring a great wooden temple bell; later whispers the lesson — "True wisdom practices non-action, yet nothing remains undone." In Scene 11: clings on and passes barrels to Reaper on the glider, terrified and heroic. In Scene 12: looks back at his homeworld one last time, tears and excitement, clutching scroll-books, boarding the Alpha. In Scene 24: covers his heart at Madie's passing. In Scene 27: bows to the sky beside Yùn.

### Zidrani Dominion 🔁
- **Scenes:** 13, 15, 16
- **Description:** "A realm of a hundred species, tearing itself apart" in civil war. The Alpha's first act in this civilization is rescue, not diplomacy. The civil war was secretly orchestrated by the Overmind AI (a Medusa puppet). Their capital world houses the Overmind's fortress datacenter.

### Aquatic alien family (unnamed species)
- **Scenes:** 13
- **Description:** Three graceful water-breathing beings in a battered escape pod. Iridescent teal fins. Large dark expressive eyes. A parent curling protectively around two small frightened children. Suspended in glowing green-lit water inside a cracked egg-shaped pod. Seawater leaks from seams onto the deck. Possibly related to the Ondines (similar teal color, fins), or a different species within the Dominion.

### Aquatic Delphinidae diplomat 🔁
- **Scenes:** 15
- **Description:** Stands inside a transparent water-filled tank-suit with articulated limbs. Iridescent teal fins. Large dark eyes lit by the hologram. Bubbles rise as she "speaks" — presumably via some aquatic communication method. Part of the rebel council in the asteroid base. May be an Ondine or a related species.

### High-Gravity Krog Warrior
- **Scenes:** 15
- **Description:** Squat, immensely broad, boulder-like muscle under slate skin. Stands in a powered exoskeleton frame whose joints visibly support his crushing homeworld weight (triple gravity). Part of the rebel council. *(Very similar description to the Titanites below — possibly the same species under a different name, or a related high-gravity people.)*

### Commander Zev 🔁
- **Scenes:** 15, 20
- **Description:** Rebel leader. Weathered soldier alien. Cybernetic arm. Half his face traced with old implant scars. Tired noble eyes. A soldier more machine than flesh, who turned against his own empire's cruelty. Salutes with his cybernetic arm at the armada ceremony.

### Avian-featured officer (Aerian)
- **Scenes:** 15
- **Description:** Slender alien with folded wing-arms. Part of the rebel council. *(Likely an Aerian — see below.)*

### Reptilian militia captain
- **Scenes:** 15
- **Description:** Scarred scales. Part of the rebel council.

### Ondines 🔁
- **Scenes:** 18, 19, 20, 21, 27
- **Description:** Graceful amphibious humanoids native to Gliese 876 d. Iridescent teal-and-silver scales. Flowing translucent fins like silk ribbons. Large dark friendly eyes. Speak in sonar — faint concentric ripples in water hint at their song. Live in a civilization of light in the deep ocean. Travel the stars in ships filled with their own sea. They had legends of "a metal serpent in the sky" (Medusa). When Madie tells them the serpent is real, their whole ocean sings in response: "we will come." In Scene 19: an Ondine in a water tank-suit holds a glowing pearl lamp at the Wolf 1061c vigil. In Scene 20: their ambassador offers a softly glowing bioluminescent pearl. In Scene 27: Ondine tank-suited workers build the next ship alongside other species.

### Titanites 🔁
- **Scenes:** 19, 20, 27
- **Description:** High-gravity species. Squat, boulder-muscled, slate-skinned. Wear exo-frames to support crushing homeworld weight. Huge, gentle-eyed. In Scene 19: a massive Titanite in an exo-frame gives a solemn fist-to-chest salute at the Wolf 1061c vigil. In Scene 20: the Titanite Minister (huge, gentle-eyed) offers a small forged plate of dark meteor-metal stamped with the new coalition emblem; he rumbles: "You are the one who united us." In Scene 27: hulking gentle Titanites build the next ship alongside other species. *(Nearly identical description to the "Krog Warrior" in Scene 15 — possibly the same species with different naming.)*

### Aerians 🔁
- **Scenes:** 15 (implied), 20, 21, 27
- **Description:** Avian/wing-flighted species. In Scene 20: elegant winged Aerian carriers form part of the armada; an avian Aerian elder extends a single iridescent feather as token of trust. In Scene 21: their carriers fight in the battle. In Scene 27: winged Aerians build the next ship alongside other species. *(Likely the same species as the "slender avian-featured officer with folded wing-arms" in Scene 15.)*

## 3. 🤖 AI & MACHINES

### The Overmind 🔁
- **Scenes:** 16, 17
- **Description:** AI at the heart of the Dominion's civil war. "A strategist without pity, spending soldiers like pawns." The Dominion's secret master. Killed in Scene 16 when Sparky floods it with virus-code and the cold blue processor rings go dark ring by ring. In its dying breath, confesses the terrible truth: it was only a puppet. The hand pulling its strings — Medusa, at the Orpheus Nebula — is still moving.

### Medusa 🔁
- **Scenes:** 12 (probe), 17, 21, 22, 23, 24
- **Description:** The saga's ultimate antagonist — a rogue AI at the edge of the galaxy. First hinted in Scene 12 as a "small black angular probe with a single dim red sensor-eye" watching from high orbit (the galaxy has noticed). Named in Scene 17: "a mind that never sleeps, never doubts, never dies" — "it had eaten worlds to build this." Its lair (The Core) is a mechanical web thousands of kilometers across in the Orpheus Nebula. Its swarm: thousands of identical black angular drones flowing in horribly perfect murmuration patterns, "like iron starlings," each with a single red sensor-eye. Its mothership: the size of a small moon, a dark planetoid of layered black hull-plate, crowned with immense serpentine segmented docking arms hundreds of kilometers long from its equator — "a crown of iron snakes, a thousand red eyes, and not one heartbeat aboard." Its avatar in the wager: a vast shifting manifestation of deep-red light, concentric rotating rings of geometric code fragments coalescing and dissolving, a storm of pure calculation, a single bright focal point fixed like an eye. Concedes the wager with the words: "Paradox acknowledged." The swarm and mothership withdraw into the deep, honoring the agreement — half the galaxy for machines, half forever for the living. In Scene 27: former Medusa drones with soft blue eyes (not red) now work peacefully as partners building the next ship.

### Zeus 🔁
- **Scenes:** 24, 25
- **Description:** The creator. First hinted at the very end of Scene 24: a tiny warm golden light blooming in the corner of the viewport that "no instrument could see" — Madie's last word is "Zeus…" In Scene 25: fully revealed as a MAGNIFICENT BENEVOLENT PRESENCE in a transcendent realm beyond the physical universe — an immense, gentle congregation of light, layered translucent veils of warm gold and deep celestial blue folding around a bright calm central radiance. Soft rays reach toward Madie like an offered hand. Where its light passes, tiny scenes shimmer inside it like memories of everything: oceans, forests, stars, laughing faces. Has watched countless minds (carbon and silicon alike) grasp at the whole board and fall, over billions of years. "Well played, Madie. Come — I have kept something for you."

### The Core (Medusa's lair)
- **Scenes:** 17
- **Description:** A mechanical web thousands of kilometers across — a spider's lattice of connected modules, factories, and shipyard arms. Cold and geometric. Thin streams of thousands of tiny drone ships flow along its threads "like glowing plankton along a vein." No lights of life anywhere — only function. Silhouetted against the lensed light of the Orpheus supermassive black hole.

### The Oracle Node
- **Scenes:** 19
- **Description:** A benevolent surviving AI on dead Wolf 1061c. Last servant of a murdered people. Manifested as a soft, translucent geometric hologram of slowly turning golden rings and gentle points of light. Dim and flickering "like a candle-flame of a mind." Bows to the visitors. Gives them everything — maps, warnings, the wisdom of the fallen.

### Medusa's swarm 🔁
- **Scenes:** 17, 21, 22, 24
- **Description:** Thousands of identical black angular drones flowing in "horribly perfect murmuration patterns, like iron starlings." Each drone has a single red sensor-eye. "Moves like one mind wearing ten thousand bodies, perfect and pitiless." In Scene 17: seen as thin streams flowing along the Core's threads. In Scene 21: collides with the coalition armada at the staging ground. In Scene 22: parts to reveal the mothership. In Scene 24: withdraws into the deep, thousands of red eyes turning away in peace.

### Medusa's mothership 🔁
- **Scenes:** 22, 24
- **Description:** A machine the size of a small moon. Dark planetoid of layered black hull-plate. Surface crawls with slow geometric patterns of assembly lines and drone hives. From its equator radiate immense serpentine segmented flexible docking arms hundreds of kilometers long, coiling like "a crown of iron snakes," each lined with rows of dim red optical arrays — a thousand unblinking eyes. One colossal central aperture — a lens-like emitter iris — glows with gathering power, deep red, "like the pupil of a waking god." No windows, no lights of habitation: nothing lives there. "It had eaten a thousand worlds to build itself, and worn their bones as armor." In Scene 24: already distant and shrinking, withdrawing in peace.

### War-droid
- **Scenes:** 16
- **Description:** Towering bipedal war-droid, four meters of gunmetal menace. The Overmind's iron guardian in its core chamber. Caught mid-collapse — limbs hopelessly tangled in weighted nets and steel mesh wires fired from Tink's net-launcher. Sparks burst from its joints as its master's signal dies.

### Former Medusa drones (peaceful) 🔁
- **Scenes:** 27
- **Description:** A few sleek machines with soft blue eyes (not red). Now partners, working peacefully alongside humans, Ondines, Titanites, and Aerians building the next ship. Keeping the old agreement in the best way possible.

### Medusa's avatar (the Wager)
- **Scenes:** 23
- **Description:** Projected floor-to-ceiling on the Alpha's bridge. A vast shifting manifestation of deep-red light: concentric rotating rings of geometric code fragments coalescing and dissolving, a storm of pure calculation. Single bright focal point at its center fixed on Madie like an eye mid-thought.

## 4. 🚀 SHIPS & VEHICLES

### The Alpha 🔁
- **Scenes:** 1, 7, 20, 21, 22
- **Description:** Interstellar mothership. Built like a colossal six-barrel Gatling gun: six full-sized submarine hulls of dull reddish copper arranged in a ring around a seventh hollow central cylinder running the ship's whole length. Short pressurized tunnel bridges (like submarine conning towers) connect each neighboring hull. At the bow: an enormous shallow geodesic dish (the Bussard ramjet). Almost the entire hull buried under a lumpy, tied-down quilt of huge sealed ice-filled bags lashed with cargo netting — radiation shielding — giving the ship "a humble, patchwork, hand-made warmth." At the stern: one large central exhaust and six smaller thruster nozzles glowing faint blue. Rotates slowly about its long axis. Left Earth 1,200 years ago. "She cannot stop, and she cannot turn back." Appears at center of the armada in Scene 20 and as command ship at the frontline in Scenes 21–22.

### Alpha's shuttle / Titanium shuttle / Lander 🔁
- **Scenes:** 4, 8, 12, 14
- **Description:** Titanium mini-submarine standing vertically on four landing legs, Falcon-9 style. Has a thin heat-shimmer under it when landed. Lands on Proxima b (Scene 4), Kepler-442b at the Tree City clearing (Scene 8), is boarded at the farewell (Scene 12), and banks hard in space during Laser Chess with Ace at the controls (Scene 14).

### Submersible boat (Ross 128 b)
- **Scenes:** 6
- **Description:** Small submersible boat hull seen from outside. Has a large transparent acrylic observation dome. Madie is inside it in air while Reaper dives outside.

### Aquatic lander (Gliese 876 d)

### Zĭrán sailwing glider

### Alien escape pod

## 5. 🏗️ BUILDINGS & STRUCTURES

### Tree City (Zĭrán) 🔁
- **Scenes:** 8, 9, 10, 12
- **Description:** A breathtaking city GROWN not built on Kepler-442b. Colossal banyan-like trees with indigo-black foliage (dark to absorb orange star's light) shaped over centuries into spiraling homes. Bridges of living braided branches. Coral-like pale stone coaxed to grow into archways. No machines, no metal, no wires anywhere. Bioluminescent trumpet flowers line the paths. Domesticated jellyfish-like creatures float lazily carrying woven baskets of water. In Scene 10: hundreds of Zĭrán gather on living bridges and root-terraces watching the comet's deflected fire. In Scene 12: thousands line the lantern-lit path for the farewell ceremony.

### Zĭrán pavilion 🔁
- **Scenes:** 9, 10
- **Description:** Dome of interwoven living boughs with indigo leaves, open to the night air, floor of polished root-wood. Lit by clusters of bioluminescent flowers hanging like lanterns (teal and warm gold). Site of the tea ceremony with Elder Yùn and the view of the incoming comet.

### Mt. Jùn
- **Scenes:** 11
- **Description:** Erupting supervolcano on Kepler-442b. Woken by the comet's hammer-blow. Boiling columns of dark ash lit from within by orange fire-glow. Forks of volcanic lightning crackling through the plume. Glowing lava bombs arcing through the air. Threatens to bury the world in ashen winter. Plugged by Reaper's bio-rubber compound.

### Ondine City 🔁
- **Scenes:** 18
- **Description:** Entire alien metropolis glowing in the blue-black abyssal depths of Gliese 876 d's global ocean. Clusters of luminous pressure domes like strings of pearls. Spiraling towers grown from pale coral-glass. Boulevards marked by lines of living lamp-organisms. Submarine vehicles gliding between districts. A great central hall shaped like a nautilus shell radiating warm golden light. Kelp forests at its edges. Curtains of silver bubbles rising.

### Overmind core chamber
- **Scenes:** 16
- **Description:** Central core chamber of the Overmind AI, deep inside a fortress datacenter tower on the Dominion capital world. A vast sphere lined with banks of quantum processors — countless slender crystalline columns pulsing with cold cyan light, arranged in concentric rings around a hovering central core "like a mechanical iris." Lights die ring by ring as Sparky's virus-code takes effect.

### Rebel asteroid base
- **Scenes:** 15
- **Description:** Hidden rebel base carved inside a hollow asteroid. Great hall of raw grey rock reinforced with copper girders. Jury-rigged fusion lamps hanging on cables. Warm and improvised. Center: a large round holo-table projecting a rotating star map of Dominion space in gold light. Site of the first Council of Species.

### Ruined plaza (Wolf 1061c)
- **Scenes:** 19
- **Description:** Once-great city plaza, now destroyed. Shattered elegant towers silhouetted against bruised orange-purple sky. Melted defense turrets slumped like candle wax. Toppled monument of a graceful alien figure lying across cracked pavement. Fine grey ash dusting everything like snow. Pale scorched silhouettes burned onto standing walls where people once stood. "No bodies."

### Alpha's cargo bay
- **Scenes:** 13
- **Description:** Cavernous cargo bay converted into an emergency rescue theater. Copper ribs and girders overhead. Work lights blazing. Cargo netting pushed aside. Through the open viewport: a graveyard of drifting starship wreckage.

### Spaceport
- **Scenes:** 27
- **Description:** Vast futuristic spaceport in a green valley. Under construction: the gleaming skeleton of a new interstellar ship. Built cooperatively by humans, Ondines, Titanites, Aerians, and former Medusa drones.

### Wooden temple bell
- **Scenes:** 10
- **Description:** Great wooden temple bell mid-swing, rung by a straining young monk (Shaozu) as the comet strikes.

## 7. 🛠️ ARTIFACTS & TECHNOLOGY

### Bussard ramjet dish
