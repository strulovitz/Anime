## PROBLEM 3 — Scene 23: Medusa's response "Paradox acknowledged" is not actually a paradox

Nir's exact words:

> why is this considered paradox ? i don't this is a paradox .

He's correct. What Madie presents to Medusa is NOT a logical paradox (a true self-contradiction). It's a **decision-theory / expected-value argument under uncertainty** — structurally identical to Pascal's Wager: there's an unknown, unquantifiable but non-zero probability of catastrophic downside risk (a "creator" punishing total conquest) weighed against a finite, calculable upside (winning the whole galaxy vs. only half). A rational agent facing that kind of asymmetric risk should hedge rather than go all-in — that's a risk-management conclusion, not the resolution of a paradox. Medusa "finding no error" and saying "Paradox acknowledged" mischaracterizes what actually happened: it should be Medusa re-running its own risk/expected-value calculation and concluding the wager isn't worth it, not discovering a contradiction in its own logic.
