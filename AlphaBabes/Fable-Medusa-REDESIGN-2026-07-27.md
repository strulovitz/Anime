THE RULING PRINCIPLE: a superintelligence's body is dictated by three laws

A machine that designs itself has no ancestors, no aesthetics, no myths. Its shape is the solution to exactly three problems:

    Heat. Every computation pays the Landauer toll — erasing bits costs energy, and that energy must leave as heat, radiated by area: P=σAT⁴. A moon-sized sphere is the single worst shape for a computer — minimum surface per volume. The snake-crown dies here, and so does the planetoid.
    Latency. Light crosses 3,000 km in 10 ms. A moon-sized mind thinking as one unit would think at the speed of a glacier. So the intelligence CANNOT be uniformly spread — physics forces a hierarchy: tiny, dense, fast cores + vast slow archives.
    Fabrication. It is a von Neumann machine — it IS its own factory. Its body is mostly supply chain: sorted feedstock, foundries, launch infrastructure.

Apply those three laws and Medusa designs herself:

MEDUSA MOTHERSHIP — redesigned from physics

A thermal onion, coldest at the heart, glowing at the skin:

    The Cryo Citadel (center, never visible from outside): the actual mind — quantum and reversible-computing cores held at millikelvin temperatures inside nested vacuum shells, like a dilution refrigerator scaled to a cathedral. Cold computing is cheap computing — the smartest place is the coldest place. A few hundred meters across. Everything else exists to serve it.
    Computronium shells: dense 3D processing lattice honeycombed with liquid-metal heat pipes, pumping the cost of thought outward.

For one-off problems (an n-body orbital solution, a blast propagation), digital simulation is wasteful — Medusa 3D-prints a crystalline analog computer whose physical behavior IS the calculation, reads the answer, and re-melts it.
