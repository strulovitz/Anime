ENTITY #19 — ZEUS (Scenes 24, 25)

Reference design sheet, multi-panel study on a deep-space background: ZEUS — a FOUR-DIMENSIONAL BEING, depicted with geometric honesty: we only ever see his 3D cross-section, the way a hand dipped through a pond's surface appears to the fish as five separate wonders moving as one. PANEL 1, REST POSE (the renderable anchor): a great TESSERACT PROJECTION — a cube suspended within a cube, joined by seamless struts — about four meters tall, with smaller smooth satellite solids arrayed around it in quiet formation: SEVERAL SEPARATE FLAWLESS FORMS that are unmistakably ONE being. PANEL 2, MATERIAL CLOSE-UP: engineered nacre with STRUCTURAL COLOR — gold and deep celestial blue iridescence produced by microscopic surface ridges, like a morpho butterfly's wing: real optics, matte, calm, and THERMALLY SILENT — no glow, no rays, no halo, ever (perfection radiates nothing). PANEL 3, the signature: at the boundary of every form the background BENDS — faint gravitational lensing, stars refracting as through curved glass around his silhouettes. PANEL 4, gesture study: one small smooth nacre form drifted apart from the rest, extending — the cross-section of a limb existing mostly in a direction we cannot point to — hovering a breath away from a human hand's fingertips. PANEL 5, rotation study: three stages of the cross-section transforming lawfully as he rotates through the fourth axis — forms merging, blooming, and receding smoothly, new geometry appearing from nowhere and returning to nowhere, without violence.

Usage notes: FORBIDDEN forever: faces, eyes, wings, robes, old men, thrones, light-rays, memory-imagery. His presence effects are exactly two: structural-color nacre + lensing. In Scene 24 only the corner-gleam + bending stars appear; the full form waits for Scene 25.
