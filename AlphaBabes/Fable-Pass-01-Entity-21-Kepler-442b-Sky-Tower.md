ENTITY #21 — THE KEPLER-442b SKY-TOWER (Scene 6)

Reference design sheet: the SKY-TOWER of Kepler-442b — a giant flyer on a 1.3g super-Earth, and NOT a pteranodon and NOT a bird: flight in heavy gravity is impossible without cheating, so evolution cheated with chemistry. Body: a long keel-frame of pneumatic hollow struts; two immense indigo membrane wings; and the design key — rows of INTERNAL HYDROGEN BUOYANCY SACS along the spine, biologically fermented hydrogen making the animal lighter than its size in the dense, thick air of the super-Earth. It does not defy its gravity; it outsmarts it. Head: a slender sensor-boom with four heat-sensing pit organs for reading volcanic thermals (no beak-face, no teeth-grin). Launch study: dropping from cliff edges to gain airspeed. Flight study: riding volcanic thermal columns, buoyancy sacs slightly swollen. Scale: wingspan wider than the crew's glider it once towed through the turbulence.
