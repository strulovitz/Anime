ENTITY #15 — MEDUSA SPY PROBE (Scene 12)

Reference design sheet, multiple views on neutral dark background: the MEDUSA SPY PROBE — a machine built for one job: never be noticed. A small faceted body, sintered matte-black photovoltaic skin (black because every photon is food), no running lights, no emissions, no markings. Its single feature: a wide telescope objective — a deep-red interference-coated lens that stares without blinking, catching starlight like a cold ember. Cutaway panel: dense storage core and a tight-beam laser emitter — it records for months in silence, then fires its observations home in a single millisecond burst, invisible to anyone not standing exactly in the beam. Panels: front, side, cutaway, and an in-situ study: the probe drifting dark against a starfield, nearly invisible, only the red objective faintly gleaming. It does not watch like a predator. It watches like a tombstone with a camera.

Usage note: in Scene 12 it must feel FOUND, not attacking — inert, patient, chilling.
