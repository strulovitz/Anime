# Entity #93 — Bioluminescent Plankton
## By Claude Fable 5 — 2026-07-27

SUBJECT: a drifting cloud of BIOLUMINESCENT PLANKTON in the lightless abyssal ocean of Gliese 876 d — underwater stars, a floating constellation of living light. Single full-frame underwater reference image.

WATER — total darkness: this is a world ocean where no sunlight has ever reached; the background is true deep blue-black, grading to absolute black at the frame's edges. There is NO surface light, NO god-rays — every photon in this image is made by a living thing.

THE PLANKTON — hard-science bioluminescence: billions of individual organisms, each a fleck a few millimeters across, producing cold chemical light (luciferin chemistry — light without heat, like Earth's dinoflagellates). Individually they are single points; together they form a vast, three-dimensional drifting CLOUD that fills the frame like a nebula underwater. Their colors: mostly cool teal and silver-white points, with sparse, rarer warm-gold individuals scattered through like different star classes — so the cloud genuinely reads as a starfield with depth.

STRUCTURE AND MOTION: the cloud is not uniform — it is shaped by invisible currents into swirls, streamers, and denser ribbons, like star-lanes in a galaxy: a bright dense band sweeping diagonally through the frame, thinning into wisps and outlying sparks. Where a current shears the cloud, the organisms flare slightly brighter (mechanically stimulated bioluminescence — physically true: they light up when the water moves against them), creating a soft traveling wave of brightness along the band's edge.

DEPTH CUES: nearest specks are soft bokeh orbs (shallow depth of field), the mid-cloud is crisp points, the far side fades into dim haze — thousands of meters of living space in one frame. A faint, barely-visible drift of marine snow falls through the scene.

MOOD: the first stars a deep-ocean civilization ever saw — silent, slow, sacred.

FORBIDDEN: no sunlight, no water surface, no creatures or city in frame, no rainbow colors — only teal, silver-white and sparse gold points against blue-black darkness.
