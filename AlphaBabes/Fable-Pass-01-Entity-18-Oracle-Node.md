ENTITY #18 — THE ORACLE NODE (Scene 19)

Reference design sheet, two-panel study: THE ORACLE NODE — the last surviving guardian AI of a murdered civilization, and the anti-Medusa in one design: it is power-starved and honest about it. PANEL 1, the body: a scarred, dust-caked projector pillar, waist-high, of once-beautiful pale alloy now pitted by ten thousand years of ash and weather, tended cables running to a few salvaged, cracked solar panels propped nearby — a survivor's trickle-feed. PANEL 2, the interface: above the pillar, a soft translucent volumetric display of slowly turning golden rings and gentle points of light — dim, and FLICKERING, because the flicker is not ghostliness, it is brownouts: the machine spending more than it has in order to speak. Gesture study: its "bow" — the projection deliberately dimming and inclining, spending its scarce watts on listening instead of shining. Mood: gentleness, loyalty, dignified poverty — a candle-flame of a mind that rationed its own consciousness for millennia, waking only when someone finally came.

Usage note: always show the physical pillar AND the projection together — the truth of the machine is the poverty of its body.
