# Entity #90 — Translucent Fish Schools
## By Claude Fable 5 — 2026-07-27

SUBJECT: a great school of small TRANSLUCENT FISH-LIKE CREATURES in the warm shallow sea of Ross 128 b — living glass, almost invisible except for the light they catch. Single full-frame underwater reference image.

WATER AND LIGHT: warm turquoise sea, shallow, with broad shafts of salmon-pink sunlight from an orange-red dwarf star slanting down from the surface as volumetric god-rays. Fine sparkling particles drift in the beams. Clean water background — the school is the subject.

THE CREATURES — alien, not Earth fish: each animal is the size of a human hand, with a smooth torpedo body of almost perfectly TRANSPARENT flesh — like a fish sculpted from clear glass. Through the body, faintly visible: a delicate silver thread of internal skeleton and a tiny cluster of pearl-like organs near the head. No scales — a glass-smooth skin surface. Two pairs of gossamer-thin fins ripple along the flanks, and a small vertical tail fan drives them. Their only strong visual feature is REFRACTION: each body bends and catches the salmon god-rays, so the school reads as thousands of living lenses — flashes of silver, rose and turquoise light with almost no visible bodies.

THE SCHOOL — collective behavior, physically true: thousands of individuals moving as one organism in a dense, coordinated bait-ball formation that fills the frame in a sweeping S-curve. Where the light shafts cross the school, whole sheets of it ignite in silver shimmer; in shadow it nearly vanishes. On one side, the formation is PARTING — flowing smoothly around an obstacle like a silver curtain drawn open, each fish turning in perfect synchrony with its neighbors.

DEEP BACKGROUND: far behind the school, in the blue haze at the edge of visibility, the faint colossal silhouette of an immense, gentle, slow filter-feeding sea creature — barely more than a shadow, out of focus, giving the school something to part around and the frame a sense of vast scale.

FORBIDDEN: no Earth fish anatomy (no scales, no goldfish/tuna/sardine look), no eyes larger than pinpoints, no cartoon fish faces — these are elegant living lenses, beautiful through light alone.
