SCENE 2 — Awakening

NARRATION:

Twelve hundred years. Empires rose and fell on the Earth they left behind — and the ten slept on, dreaming in the frost, while the ship's silent robots kept watch. Then, one morning older than any morning in history, the pods opened. Madie woke first — commander, engineer, daughter of Romania. She had crossed more time than any human being who ever lived. Her first thought was not fear. It was: we made it.
