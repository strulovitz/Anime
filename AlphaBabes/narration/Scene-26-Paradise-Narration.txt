SCENE 26 — Paradise 💖

NARRATION:

And this was the gift the creator had kept for her. Not a throne. Not a monument. Something infinitely better: the small, enormous life she had always wanted. A hillside in Romania. Her family waving from the porch, borscht on the table. And beside her — Nir, her love, her home in human form — with a scrapbook full of every dream they'd promised each other: the roar of Formula 1, the quiet temples of Japan, Monet's water lilies in Paris, the whole world crossed hand in hand, two happy nomads with nowhere to be and forever to get there. Her sisters sail on among the stars, and they will never know. But somewhere beyond the sky they guard, Madie is laughing in the sunlight. She saved the universe. The universe said thank you — properly.
