SCENE 9 — Tea with Elder Yùn

NARRATION:

Elder Yùn taught them the word: wu wei — actionless action. Do only what the river of nature asks; force nothing. It had given his people ten centuries of peace. Madie drank the tea and asked the engineer's question: 'And when the river floods — do you not build a dam?' The old monk's dapples brightened into slow, spreading rings of quiet joy — the Zĭrán smile — and he did not answer. Above the pavilion, unseen, a new star had appeared in the night. It had a tail. And it was coming.
