SCENE 27 — Epilogue & Dedication

NARRATION:

The war never came back. The machines kept their half of the sky, and kept their word — and in time, they even helped build the next ship, for the next voyage, toward a new whisper of life on a far-off water world called K2-18b. On a green hill above the launch field, nine sisters planted a tree for their tenth. They talked to her photo. They left her a bowl of borscht. And as the first stars came out, Ace raised her hand in salute — and at that exact moment, past the brightest star in the sky, something streaked by like a wink. 'She'd say we're behind schedule,' Ace laughed, wiping her eyes. Mariner smiled at the sky: 'Then let's fly.' And the story — like all the best stories — did not end. It set course.
