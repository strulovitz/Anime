SCENE 25 — Meeting Zeus

NARRATION:

She opened her eyes... and there was no pain, and no ship, and no war. There was a place outside every place — still, warm, patient — and far below her, small enough to see whole, hung the universe she had saved: every galaxy a grain of glittering dust inside a sphere of glass. And before her stood the one she had wagered everything on. Not a face. Not a light. Several flawless forms of gold and blue, moving in perfect agreement — the way a hand dipped into a pond appears, to the fish below, as five separate wonders moving as one. Around him, the stars politely bent. For billions of years he had watched countless minds, carbon and silicon alike, grasp at the whole board and fall. She was the first to reason her way to him — not through faith, not through fear, but through a wager laid down with a steady hand to save every living thing. One small, smooth form drifted close to her fingertips — almost, almost touching. And he spoke without words: 'Well played, Madie. Come — I have kept something for you.' Behind her, a door of ordinary sunlight stood open. Through it: green hills. A summer sky. Home.
