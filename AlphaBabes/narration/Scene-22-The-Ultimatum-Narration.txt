SCENE 22 — The Ultimatum (fully rebuilt on the thermodynamic doctrine)

NARRATION:

It was the size of a moon — and it had no shape. Structure without end, fading into haze, so that their sensors could not even tell them where it began. It had eaten a thousand worlds to build itself — and there they hung along its flank: sorted into mountains, iron with iron, copper with copper, whole planets reduced to inventory. Its red glow was not fury; machines have none. It was waste heat — the exhaust of a mind that never stops calculating, shining off curtains of molten rain. The crew called it by the name they had given it long ago: Medusa. Its voice filled every ship at once, flat and infinite: 'Surrender your biological lives for termination — or be destroyed.' The fleet held its breath. And at the very front, smallest of all, one copper ship did not retreat a single meter. On its bridge, a young woman from Romania closed her eyes... and remembered an old story about a captain, a bluff, and a game that isn't chess.
