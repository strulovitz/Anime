SCENE 20 — The Grand Armada

NARRATION:

They came from oceans and from crushing gravity, from cloud-cities and from ruins — hundreds of ships, dozens of peoples, one purpose. And when the time came to choose who would lead them all, no one voted, no one argued. One by one, the leaders of worlds crossed the bridge of the little copper ship from Earth and laid their tokens in the hands of a young woman from Romania. 'I'm just an engineer,' Madie protested. The Titanite Minister rumbled his answer: 'No. You are the one who united us.' She accepted — with a steady voice, a straight back... and, waiting at her station, a bowl of borscht her sisters had made her, so that home would stand beside her at the end of the universe.
