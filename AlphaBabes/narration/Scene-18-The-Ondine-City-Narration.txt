SCENE 18 — The Ondine City

NARRATION:

The war against Medusa could not be won alone — so the Alpha went from star to star, knocking on the doors of every civilization brave enough to answer. The first door was under an ocean. The Ondines: a people of song and light, whose cities are pearls strung across the deep, who speak in sonar and travel the stars in ships filled with their own sea. They had legends of a metal serpent in the sky. When Madie told them the serpent was real — they did not hide. They sang. And their whole ocean sang with them: we will come.
