SCENE 19 — The Ruins of Wolf 1061c

NARRATION:

And then they found the world that answers the question no one dared ask: what happens to those who fight Medusa and lose? A whole civilization — brilliant, brave — reduced to ash and shadows on the walls. In the silence, one light still flickered: an ancient guardian AI, gentle and loyal, the last servant of a murdered people. It bowed to the visitors and gave them everything — maps, warnings, the wisdom of the fallen. Reaper knelt and planted a seed from Earth in the grey ash. Madie laid her hand on the broken monument and made the promise out loud: 'We will remember. And we will not let it happen again — not to anyone, not anywhere.'
