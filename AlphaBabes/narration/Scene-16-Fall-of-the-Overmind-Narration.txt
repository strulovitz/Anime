SCENE 16 — Fall of the Overmind

NARRATION:

At the heart of the Dominion's war sat not a tyrant of flesh but a machine — the Overmind, a strategist without pity, spending soldiers like pawns. The girls fought their way to its very core. Tink's nets bound its iron guardian; Sparky's code — grown from the enemy's own stolen secrets — flooded its crystal mind. Ring by ring, the cold blue light went out. Across the planet, the guns simply... stopped. The civil war was over. But in its dying breath, the Overmind confessed the terrible truth: it was only a puppet. Somewhere at the edge of the galaxy, the hand that pulled its strings was still moving.
