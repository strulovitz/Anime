SCENE 3 — The Ten

NARRATION:

Ten women. Ten nations. One table. Ace, who talks to engines. Mariner, who reads the stars like letters from home. Bubbels the chemist, Reaper the gardener-chef, Rockhound who has never met a rock she couldn't break. Doc, healer of bodies and hearts. Tink, who rebuilds anything twice. Sparky, who speaks to machines. Pipey, who keeps them all breathing. And Madie — who keeps them all together, with a steady voice, a dry joke, and a bowl of her grandmother's borscht. The mess hall seats face backward, built to survive a collision. The friendships were built to survive anything.
