SCENE 13 — Wreckage and Mercy

NARRATION:

The song had led them across the dark — into a war. The Zidrani Dominion: a realm of a hundred species, tearing itself apart. The Alpha's first act in this new civilization was not diplomacy. It was pulling a drowning family out of the wreckage — water-breathers, their world in a cracked shell of glass. Doc pressed her hand to the glass, and a small hand of tendrils rose to meet it. Whatever flag they flew, whatever blood or water ran in them — the crew of the Alpha had chosen their side: the living.
