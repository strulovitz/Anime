SCENE 5 — First Life

NARRATION:

They had come to escape a flare — and found, sheltering in the same cave, the answer to humanity's oldest question. Life. Not tall, not proud — a patch of lichen no bigger than a hand, glowing softly in the dark, enduring the fury of its star underground, just as they did. Reaper's hand trembled above it. No one spoke. Somewhere behind their eyes, every scientist who ever lived was weeping. We were never alone. Life finds a way — here, and everywhere.
