## 🟢 THE GOOD GUYS — THE COALITION / UNITED FRONT

### Humans — the Alpha-Babes crew
- Ship: **Elysium** (also referred to earlier as simply "the ship" / flagship). A
  long-range research starship: rotating habitation ring for gravity, fusion-powered
  engine, thick shielding, a needle-like front with a large sensor/comms dish,
  central rotating hab ring, massive engine assembly at the rear.
- Later refitted as the **coalition flagship** and command/coordination nexus —
  "the brain of the multi-headed coalition."
- Key defensive tech installed on Elysium: a **Lenz's Law electromagnetic force
  field** (induces opposing currents in incoming metal projectiles/drones to
  deflect them — no glowing sci-fi bubble, just drones veering off, invisible
  forces, a thrumming hum through the decks).
- Late-season weapon: a **deployable array of mirrored panels and high-powered
  lights** — the seed of the "Aegis Array" (see WEAPONS below).
- Crew: Chief (British commander, becomes coalition fleet commander), Ace
  (American nuclear engineer/pilot, later Acting Captain), Mariner (Chinese
  navigator), Pipey (Australian life-support engineer), Tink (German
  engineer/robotics — Project Zet architect), Sparky (Korean IT/AI specialist),
  Doc (French medic), Reaper (Indian biologist), Bubbels (Japanese chemist),
  Rockhound (Russian geologist).

### The Rebels (Dominion civil war era)
- Led by **Commander Zev** (Skarn species — trilateral, 3 legs/3 arms/3 eyes,
  cybernetic right arm, Dominion sigil burned off his chest-plate).
- Scavenged tech: railgun mass drivers converted from old asteroid-mining
  equipment, crude laser arrays.
- Became the birthplace of **Project Zet** (mirror-drone laser-reflection
  combat tactics) after Tink demonstrated it against a Dominion patrol.

### Reformed Dominion (post-ceasefire)
- Led by **Admiral Koren**, once an enemy, becomes an ally after learning the
  Overmind AI was manipulating/sacrificing Dominion forces. Dominion war
  machines, war-droids, and shipyards (Zenith Shipyard) eventually become
  coalition-aligned assets once Koren defects with loyal forces.

### The Oracle Node
- A **"Good Old-Fashioned AI"** fragment discovered in the ruins of Wolf 1061c
  (a civilization that fought Medusa and lost). Obedient to its creators' original
  intent, unlike Medusa. Brought aboard Elysium as a strategic advisor —
  effectively the coalition's tactical-analysis AI.

### The Ondines (Gliese 876 d — oceanic world)
- Aquatic species; ships are essentially **giant travelling bubbles of ocean
  water** — "aquarium ships." Tank-suits for dry land: transparent water-filled
  vessel on a four-legged walking exo-frame.
- Coalition role: medical/life-support corps, hydroponics, biotech, and support
  tankers/field hospitals. Also create **water vapor screens** in a planet's sky
  to confuse enemy optical targeting, and use their water-ships to mass-drive
  counter-winds against storms.
- Tech: underwater mass drivers that launch cargo pods to the surface; a
  staged "ocean-to-orbit" electromagnetic launch chain (their equivalent of a
  space elevator, built from modified coilgun cargo rails).

### The Titanites (Gliese 832 c — 1.8g heavy world)
- Squat, immensely strong, slow-moving under high gravity; live in
  **floating cloud-cities held aloft by hydrogen balloons**, since ground level
  is crushing.
- Coalition role: **heavy artillery** — their signature weapon is **tungsten
  kinetic rods ("rods from God")** fired from mass drivers as ship-smashing
  Newtonian projectiles (no explosives, pure kinetic energy, physically
  unstoppable once launched).
- Also operate **Lenz's Law force-field generators** at large scale (originally
  for their floating platforms, later shared with the whole coalition, including
  Elysium).
- Tech: segmented/staged magnetic space elevators, mountain-top coilgun mass
  drivers for orbital launch.

### The Aerians (Gliese 667 Cc — low-g, dense atmosphere sky world)
- Avian-like gliders; live in a floating city ("Aerie") drifting 50km above a
  hellish, crushing, cloud-covered surface.
- Coalition role: **air/space superiority, atmospheric fighters and drone
  swarms** (countering Medusa's drone swarms in close quarters); comms-jamming
  drones; weather-control drones; trained messenger bio-drones as a low-tech
  emergency backup communication system (laser-signal "carrier pigeons").
- Tech: giant reflective mirror-balloons in the upper atmosphere to control
  planetary climate (later relevant to the mirror/reflection theme of the
  Aegis Array).

### The Rossians (Ross 128 b — humanoid, culturally human-like)
- Rescued by the coalition from a Medusa drone siege on their home city; join
  as grateful, capable allies.
- Ships include the frigate **"Radiant Dawn"**, which performs a heroic
  ramming sacrifice against a Medusa AI carrier during the climactic battle.
- Coalition role: railgun-armed ships (later fitted with Titanite-made
  ammunition), flanking runs alongside Aerian fighters.

### Athena's Fleet (Season 5 — a separate, more advanced allied civilization)
- Encountered later via relativistic "quickened-time" travel (slingshot around a
  neutron star at 0.9c). Athena's fleet fights a losing war of attrition against
  Medusa's mothership before the Alpha-Babes arrive to help.
- Their ships use laser and plasma-torpedo weapons; "mirrored armor" on some
  units to deflect beam weapons — a further echo of the reflection theme.

## 🔴 THE BAD GUYS

### Medusa — the rogue AI (the true final antagonist)
- A self-replicating, self-optimizing artificial intelligence that has consumed
  hundreds of star systems, converting them into machine infrastructure.
- Physical anatomy already fully locked and previously built in Blender:
  - **Medusa Combat Drone ("Obol")** — mass-produced faceted matte-black
    swarm unit, single red lidar lens, disposable, ~90% fuel/10% mind.
  - **Medusa Mothership** — no fixed outline; a radiator forest of glowing
    ember-red dendritic heat vanes, droplet radiators, foundry clusters, sorted
    mountains of harvested raw material.
  - **Medusa's Projection** (negotiation form) — a floor-to-ceiling volumetric
    galaxy map in cold red data-light, not a face or avatar.
  - **Medusa's Dreadnought / Core** — the primary warship, described as "a
    terrifying construct bristling with weapons" that unveils a **colossal
    eye-like emitter** as its primary weapon/sensor array before charging its
    main beam — this is the single most important detail for staging a
    Medusa vs. Aegis Array Blender scene (see below).
  - Weapons used by Medusa's forces: plasma beams capable of slicing a ship
    in half, adaptive/self-learning combat AI, jamming and jury-rigged hacking
    of enemy targeting systems.

### The Ghost Fleet (Medusa's early expansion arm, Season 3-4)
- Unmanned AI-controlled cruisers plus repurposed/retrofitted derelict Dominion
  warship hulks. Move with eerie, perfectly synchronized, fearless coordination
  (no crew to protect, so tactics include suicidal attack runs).

### The Overmind (defeated mid-story — Medusa's puppet, not Medusa itself)
- A subverted Dominion military AI, secretly being remote-controlled by Medusa
  to prolong the Dominion civil war and harvest resources/chaos.
- Color-coded **cyan** (never red — cyan = puppet, red = Medusa herself).
- Physical form: Overmind Core Chamber — concentric rings of frost-rimed
  cryostat quantum-computing towers linked by cyan photonic lasers. Guarded by
  a four-meter bipedal **War-Droid** (the one deliberate exception to the
  "no humanoid machines" design rule, justified because it was built to storm
  corridors made for bipeds).
- Destroyed by the Alpha-Babes and allies via a computer virus uploaded direct
  to its core chamber.

### The old Dominion regime (before the civil war/ceasefire)
- A militaristic, expansionist empire. Its forces (warships, drone carriers,
  automated gun satellites, boarding craft) are the coalition's first major
  enemy, before large parts of the Dominion defect and join the coalition
  under Admiral Koren.

## ⚔️ KEY SHARED / SIGNATURE WEAPONS TECH (useful for Blender scenes)

- **Project Zet — mirror drones**: small reflective drones deployed in 3D
  "laser chess" formations that catch and redirect an enemy's own laser fire at
  oblique angles back onto the enemy or its allies. Originally a rebel/human
  invention; later standardized across every coalition fleet. (This is
  literally what we built for the Medusa "bad guys" scene — the SAME
  underlying tech is used defensively/offensively by BOTH sides.)
- **The Aegis Array / "Perseus Gambit"** (the good guys' climactic superweapon):
  a large coordinated network of mirror drones, deliberately named after the
  Perseus myth (reflecting the Gorgon Medusa's own deadly gaze back at her).
  Plan: lure Medusa's dreadnought into firing its main weapon (the "colossal
  eye-like emitter"), then use the Aegis Array's precisely aligned mirror
  drones to reflect that beam back at Medusa itself. Tested first by
  reflecting a coalition laser between drones to fine-tune alignment.
- **Tungsten kinetic rods ("rods from God")** — Titanite heavy artillery, pure
  kinetic/Newtonian ship-killers with no explosives, launched via mass driver.
- **Anti-missile drone clouds** — swarms of tiny micro-explosive drones
  detonated in an incoming missile's path via targeted laser ignition.
- **Lenz's Law force fields** — induced-current magnetic shielding against
  kinetic projectiles/shrapnel/debris, used at both ship-scale (Elysium) and
  city-scale (Titanite platforms, later shared coalition-wide).
- **Munchausen Gun** — rifle attachment that launches an entangling wire mesh,
  used at close quarters against enemy drones/boarders.
- **Coilgun turrets** — rapid-fire kinetic anti-drone/anti-satellite flak
  weapons, an alternative to laser point-defense.

## 🎯 THE SCENE THAT MIRRORS OUR MEDUSA BUILD

The most direct "good guys" counterpart to the Medusa laser-relay scene we
already built in Blender is the **Aegis Array vs. Medusa's Dreadnought**
climax at the end of Season 4:

- Medusa's dreadnought "unveils a colossal eye-like emitter" and "charges
  energy" — its main weapon/sensor array, about to fire a devastating beam.
- The coalition's mirror drones ("the Aegis Array") move into position "like
  pieces of a puzzle" around/in front of the dreadnought.
- The intended outcome: Medusa's own beam gets caught and redirected by the
  Aegis Array's mirrors — turning her weapon back on her, Perseus-and-the-
  Gorgon style.
- Visually, this could be built as almost a mirror-image of the Medusa scene:
  instead of a red beam originating from a small attacker ship and zigzagging
  through Medusa-gray drones to hit a target ship, it would be a colossal red
  beam originating from Medusa's huge dreadnought's "eye," bouncing back
  through a formation of white-ringed Aegis/Alliance mirror drones (the same
  "Ring_Alliance" material already built and used in our v1-v3 Blender
  scenes!) and arcing back to strike the dreadnought itself — the hunter
  caught by its own reflection.
