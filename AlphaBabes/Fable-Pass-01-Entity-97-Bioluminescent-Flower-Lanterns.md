# Entity #97 — Bioluminescent Flower Lanterns
## By Claude Fable 5 — 2026-07-27

SUBJECT: the FLOWER-LANTERNS of the Zĭrán farewell ceremony — hovering paper-like lanterns that carry a living glowing flower instead of a flame. Single full-frame reference image: one lantern large and crisp in the foreground, a drifting field of dozens more receding into the night sky behind it.

THE LANTERN — metal-free Zĭrán craft, physically buoyant: a delicate lantern the size of a human head, its frame woven from whisper-thin cane splints lashed with fine silk cord, paneled with translucent pale plant-parchment like handmade paper, softly wrinkled and imperfect. Instead of fire, its heart is a single BIOLUMINESCENT FLOWER — a small trumpet-bloom glowing warm GOLD with a cool TEAL throat — seated in a tiny woven cradle at the lantern's center, its light shining through the parchment panels so the whole lantern becomes a soft paper moon of mixed gold-and-teal glow. Lift comes from biology, not flame: crowning the lantern is a grown seed-pod BUOYANCY BLADDER — a translucent, iridescent sphere of living membrane filled with self-fermented hydrogen (the same trick this world's floating jellyfish use), from which the lantern hangs by four silk threads. No fire, no heat, no metal anywhere.

PHYSICAL HONESTY: each lantern glows from within with correct soft falloff; nearby lanterns faintly light each other's paper surfaces; each drifts at its own slight tilt and rotation on the night breeze — an honest scatter, not a rigid formation.

COMPOSITION AND SKY: the hero lantern floats just off-center in sharp focus, its parchment weave, silk knots and glowing flower fully detailed. Behind and beyond it, dozens more lanterns drift upward and away in a loose winding river of gold-teal lights, shrinking to soft bokeh points against a deep indigo night sky with two small silvery moons and sharp stars.

MOOD: a farewell made of light — ten thousand goodbyes rising gently, sorrow and gratitude in the same breath.

FORBIDDEN: no fire, no candles, no smoke, no metal or wire, no Chinese-lantern red — this is gold-and-teal living light in handmade paper.
