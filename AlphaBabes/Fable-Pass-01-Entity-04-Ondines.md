EVOLUTIONARY LOGIC (drives every detail): the Ondines evolved in the lightless crushing depths of a global ocean on Gliese 876 d. No sunlight ever reached them — so SOUND became their sight, and they grew their own light. No seafloor legs, no land, no fire: a civilization of swimmers who build by growing coral-glass and speak in sonar and glow.

BODY PLAN: a living bell — the main body is a graceful, muscular MANTLE shaped like an elongated closed tulip of translucent flesh, iridescent teal shading to silver at the edges, smooth and hydrodynamic. Propulsion: a directional JET SIPHON at the mantle's rear pole (visible as an elegant adjustable nozzle of soft tissue) for speed, plus two long RIBBON-FINS running the mantle's length like the undulating skirt of a cuttlefish — for slow, precise, hovering movement. They fly through water the way thought moves: silent, weightless, exact.

MANIPULATION: at the mantle's front pole, a CROWN OF TWELVE TENTACLES in two rings — an outer ring of eight long, strong, ribbon-like arms with translucent silk-fine trailing edges (the "flowing fins like silk ribbons" seen from afar), and an inner ring of four short, fine PRECISION TENDRILS that split at their tips into dozens of hair-thin feelers, capable of watchmaker-delicate work: weaving, carving coral-glass, operating instruments. Show one arm holding a glowing ceremonial orb.

THE SONAR DOME (their "face" that is not a face): crowning the front pole above the tentacles sits a smooth, beautiful acoustic MELON — a translucent dome of pale silver flesh through which faint inner structures glimmer. This is how they see: they paint the world in sound. When an Ondine "looks" at you, the dome inclines toward you and you feel a gentle harmless thrum. Their sonar speech is visible underwater as faint concentric ripple-rings pulsing from the dome. Below the dome, a ring of FOUR SMALL EYES — simple, dark, pupil-less spheres evolved late and only for nearby light: dim-sighted but tuned to one thing above all, the glow of other Ondines.

LIGHT — their soul: constellations of PHOTOPHORES (bioluminescent light-organs) run in elegant lines along the mantle, ribbon-fins and arms — pinpoints of cool teal, silver-white and occasional warm gold. This is the second half of their language: sonar carries the words, light carries the feeling. Calm = slow silver waves; joy = quick golden sparkles cascading down the ribbons; grief = all lights dimming to a single slow pulse. Show a small annotated strip of three emotion-patterns.

TANK-SUIT vignette (for land, vigil and bridge scenes): a transparent, water-filled acrylic vessel shaped to the Ondine's bell body, mounted on a graceful four-legged walking exo-frame of pale alloy; the tentacle crown reaches into flexible liquid-filled sleeve-gauntlets to manipulate the dry world; small silver bubbles rise inside; the sonar dome glows softly near the top like a lantern in a lighthouse.

MOOD OF THE DESIGN: grace, welcome and song — a being of light in the dark, utterly alien and instantly beautiful, moving like silk in slow wind. Pixar-level appeal through flow, glow and gentle inclination — never through human features. Realistic bio-materials, hard-science-fiction plausibility.
