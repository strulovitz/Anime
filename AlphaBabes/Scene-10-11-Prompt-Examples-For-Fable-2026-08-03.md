- `Fable-Pass-01-Entity-28-Tree-City.png` (the Zĭrán tree city setting)

**Full prompt used:**

Pixar/Disney 3D animation style, cinematic feature-film quality render. Warm, charming, emotionally expressive characters with the signature polished "Pixar finish" — but NOT a toy world: realistic proportions, real physical materials (brushed copper, scuffed metal, woven fabric, ice, dust), physically accurate lighting with soft global illumination, shallow cinematic depth of field. Hard science fiction: every technical detail scientifically plausible, no fantasy glow, no magic. Original design — must not resemble any existing anime or movie franchise. 16:9 widescreen, ultra-detailed, movie-still composition.

IMPORTANT CHARACTER STYLE: All human characters in this scene must be rendered in cute Pixar/Disney 3D animated cartoon style — warm, charming, emotionally expressive faces with the signature polished Pixar look, cute rounded features, animated cartoon proportions.

REFERENCE IMAGES ATTACHED — the Zĭrán aliens, the tree city setting, and the human face shown in the reference images MUST be reproduced faithfully.

Epic dawn scene on Kepler-442b, viewed from the grounds of the Zĭrán tree city from the reference. The sky is a colossal drama: dozens of blazing meteor trails streak down through a shimmering veil of high-altitude vapor (a deliberate ice-cloud screen sparkling like an artificial aurora) — and the trails visibly CURVE, bending away from the city at impossible angles, deflected mid-fall, missing the valley. High above, a single bright dot with a faint blue engine flare: the crew's ship holding position, generating an invisible magnetic shield (no beams, no glow — the physics is invisible; only the bending trails betray it). One distant fragment impacts beyond the far mountains with a rising column of smoke and a flattened orange flash. Foreground: hundreds of Zĭrán from the reference gathered on the living bridges and root-terraces — fully NON-HUMANOID low hexapods (no human faces, no bipedal bodies), parents sheltering their small young beneath the safe arch of their own bodies, elders steadying themselves on all four walking limbs — every sensory crown in the crowd raised toward the burning sky, eye-stalks converged upward, and across a hundred dappled skins the same story flashes in living light: jagged fear-patterns melting into wide, bright, expanding rings of awe as the burning stones swerve away. A great wooden temple bell mid-swing, rung by a straining young Zĭrán monk hauling the braided bell-rope with both trunk-like manipulator arms, all six limbs braced. Among the crowd: MADIE (busty, curvy, pretty, Romanian, long loose dark-brown hair, nothing on her head, ROMANIA patch, "MADIE" name tag) standing firm on a root-terrace, one hand resting protectively on the lowered sensory crown of a frightened Zĭrán child pressed against her leg — its little dapples flickering with fear — her other hand pressing a comm-earpiece, calling the maneuvers with fierce calm; beside her ELDER YÙN from the reference watches the curving fire, and down his aged silvery skin the dapple-patterns stream slowly downward like falling leaves — the Zĭrán way of weeping with joy. Camera: start on the crowd's upturned faces, tilt up across the curving meteor trails to the tiny guardian star of the ship. Mood: terror transforming into impossible hope — a sky on fire, bent aside by friendship.

REFERENCE IMAGES ATTACHED — the Zĭrán alien (Shaozu) shown in the reference image MUST be reproduced faithfully: copy his exact hexapod body shape, dappled algae-skin patterns, five-frond sensory crown, woven moss-fiber mantle, and trunk-like manipulator arms.

High-altitude action scene above the erupting supervolcano Mt. Jùn on Kepler-442b. The sky is apocalyptic: boiling columns of dark ash lit from within by orange fire-glow, forks of volcanic lightning crackling through the plume, glowing lava bombs arcing through the air trailing sparks. Cutting across this inferno: a graceful Zĭrán sailwing glider of woven fiber and pale silk membrane, towed by a magnificent tamed flying creature — a pteranodon-like beast with indigo leathery wings and gentle golden eyes, straining bravely through the turbulence. Riding the glider's open frame: REAPER (busty, curvy, pretty, Indian, mint-green chignon whipping in the hot wind, India flag patch, goggles pushed up, face streaked with ash, expression of absolute determination) heaving a wooden barrel over the side — the barrel tumbles down toward the glowing caldera below, one of several already falling, each trailing a rope of pale sticky bio-rubber compound. In the caldera beneath: earlier barrels have burst across the lava vent, spreading into a vast honeycomb of hardening grey foam that visibly chokes the eruption — the volcano's roar being smothered by a growing solid cap. Behind Reaper on the glider's open frame, the young Zĭrán monk SHAOZU from the reference — a fully NON-HUMANOID low hexapod (no human face, no bipedal body) — clings to the woven struts with all four walking limbs, their wide splayed grip-pads locked onto the frame, while his two boneless trunk-like manipulator arms heave the next barrel across to Reaper. His woven moss-fiber mantle is cinched tight against the hot wind; his five-frond sensory crown is flattened back, one amber eye-stalk fixed on Reaper, two tracking the falling barrels below. Across his dappled algae-skin race jagged, flickering fear-patterns — and yet every limb holds steady: terrified and heroic at once. A near-miss lava bomb streaks past the wingtip. Lighting: hellish orange from below, cold ash-grey above, rim-light from lightning. Camera: wide shot showing the glider, the volcano, and the falling barrels. Mood: a far-away alien sky burning above two very different friends doing the one thing nobody expected to work.
