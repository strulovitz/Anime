# Entity #100 — Small Wheeled Robot
## By Claude Fable 5 — 2026-07-27

SUBJECT: the Alpha's SMALL WHEELED UTILITY ROBOT — a knee-high maintenance and inspection rover that patrols the mothership's decks. Single full-frame reference image: three-quarter view on riveted deck plating in a copper-walled corridor, soft warm interior work-lighting, shallow depth of field. The robot is the sole subject.

CHASSIS — honest engineering, the size of a large suitcase: a low rectangular body of scuffed grey alloy with rounded corners, riding on FOUR independent wheels, each on its own visible suspension arm (rocker linkages, coil springs, honest mechanical detail) with chunky treaded rubber tires worn smooth at the centers. The body shell carries stenciled unit markings, a worn cargo barcode, small vents, and years of dings, paint chips and one mismatched replacement panel — a working tool, loved but not pampered.

HEAD — appeal without a face: from the front of the chassis rises a slender telescoping SENSOR MAST, jointed like a desk lamp, ending in a single round sensor pod: one main camera lens with an iris-ring that catches the light, flanked by two tiny status LEDs (steady green) and a small spotlight. The mast is tilted upward and slightly to one side in an unmistakably curious posture — all its charm comes from body language and the gleam of that one lens, never from a face. NO eyes, NO mouth, NO humanoid features.

TOOLS: folded flush along one flank, a small multi-jointed manipulator arm ending in a two-finger gripper with rubber pads; on the other flank, a row of tool sockets holding a probe, a brush, and a torque driver. A short whip antenna at the rear. On the deck beside it, its small open service hatch reveals tidy internals: battery pack, cable looms, cooling fins.

LIGHTING AND MOOD: warm practical corridor light from above, the robot's own small spotlight throwing a modest pool ahead of it. Mood: diligent, humble, quietly endearing — the ship's smallest crew member, always on duty.

FORBIDDEN: no humanoid shape, no face, no cartoon eyes, no resemblance to any famous movie robot — original working machine, charm through posture and craft only.
