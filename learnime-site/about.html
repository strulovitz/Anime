It's all real. 🔎

This is the promise underneath everything on this site: the science in the episodes is real science. The history is researched history — honestly labeled where historians disagree and where the story invents. The recipes on the Make Your Own page are the actual recipes we use, failures included. The progress reports never pretend. When we don't know something, we say so — the way this very website was built, mistakes admitted out loud.

Our dreams 🌍

A world where a kid in any country, on any budget, can open a free recipe and start building her own anime that same evening. Where science sneaks into hearts through stories. Where thousands of small contributions grow into universes nobody could have built alone — like Wikipedia, but for anime.
